ToS compliance, cease-and-desist runbooks, and legal exposure triage are not engineering problems. Plans that frame ToS as a robots.txt allowlist problem without legal backing are treating a legal judgment as an engineering task. Require explicit legal sign-off or owner-as-approver model with documented authority for each decision.
