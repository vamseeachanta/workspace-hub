Daily-today is a real, working scheduled task (06:00 daily). Weekly-today does NOT exist in config/scheduled-tasks/schedule-tasks.yaml; proposals suggest scheduling it at 06:05 Mondays to avoid daily-today collision. Do not assume weekly reporting exists unless it appears in canonical YAML.
