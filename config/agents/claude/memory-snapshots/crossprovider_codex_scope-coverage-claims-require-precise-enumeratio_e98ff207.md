Technical handoffs must ground coverage as 'N of M items checked' (e.g., '102 of 133 first-level entries') and explicitly state what was NOT assessed (nested repos, other systems). Broad claims like 'all machines' or 'everywhere' are commonly overstated and create false confidence.
