Grinding and ultrasonic/hammer peening parameters for fatigue improvement appear consistently across multiple ABS offshore standards: minimum depth 0.2–0.5mm, maximum 2mm or 7% of plate thickness (whichever is smaller). These tolerances are technical baselines, not one-off guidance—flagging repeated specifications across documents indicates consolidation opportunities.
