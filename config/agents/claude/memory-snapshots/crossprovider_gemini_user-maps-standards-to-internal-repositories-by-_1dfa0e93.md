Technical standards are ingested and classified by discipline (structural, cathodic-protection, pipeline, marine, installation, energy-economics, materials, regulatory, drilling) and mapped to internal repos (digitalmodel, worldenergydata, assethold, lng-a, OGManufacturing, mkt-a). Mapping is selective; not all repos are relevant to all standards.
