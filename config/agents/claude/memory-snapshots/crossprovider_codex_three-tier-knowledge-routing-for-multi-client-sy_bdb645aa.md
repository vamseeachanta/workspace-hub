Raw client documents remain at source locations; processed client-specific knowledge routes to `llm-wiki-<client>` repositories; only deduplicated and de-identified knowledge routes to canonical `llm-wiki`. This pattern prevents raw proprietary data leakage while enabling reuse of generalizable findings across clients.
