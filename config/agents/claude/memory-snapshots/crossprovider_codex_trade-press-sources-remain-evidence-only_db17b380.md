Industry publications (OGJ Worldwide Production, broker research notes) rank below primary regulator/operator sources and technical literature. These sources cannot drive conversion factors; they remain evidence-only and must be labeled `source_class: industry_technical_article` with `accepted_for_conversion: false`.
