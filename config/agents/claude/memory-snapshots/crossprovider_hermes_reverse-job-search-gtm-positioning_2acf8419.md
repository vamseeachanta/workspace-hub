Instead of 'we offer services X, Y, Z', scan open job roles to identify consulting gaps (e.g., companies hiring mooring engineers → position AI mooring analysis consulting). Then build outreach around market demand, not feature list. More effective than feature-first sales and naturally aligns consulting offerings to where companies are busy and understaffed.
