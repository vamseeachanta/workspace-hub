Centralizing fragmented policy (WRK location, spec paths, skill ownership) across an ecosystem requires 3 distinct phases: (1) hygiene gates in warn-mode on changed files, (2) pilot wave on smallest corpus with dry-run manifest approval, (3) blocking enforcement after pilot validates. Attempting all-repos enforcement on day-one risks silent propagation failures.
