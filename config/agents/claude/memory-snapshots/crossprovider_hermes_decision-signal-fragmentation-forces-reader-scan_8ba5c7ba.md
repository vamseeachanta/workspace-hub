Reports that scatter decision-relevant interpretation (posture, activity status, debt status) across executive summary, global sections, and per-provider sections force readers to scan multiple locations to prioritize action. Consolidate via a compact interpretation summary derived from existing computed fields, collocating all per-entity decision signals in one section.
