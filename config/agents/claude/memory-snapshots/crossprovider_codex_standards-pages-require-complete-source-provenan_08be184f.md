Consistent frontmatter across standards pages must include: `source_file` (path), `license_status` (encrypted/copy-permitted/licensed-use-stamp), `canonical_url` (publisher-of-record), `source_edition_verified` (edition from PDF), `visibility` (private-llm-wiki). Missing fields cause ambiguity on licensing/redistribution rights and version truth, leading to future compliance and citation defects.
