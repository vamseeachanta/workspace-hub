Observed 2026-07-28 by diffing a ~900-word draft I wrote against the ~150-word version Vamsee actually sent to a prospect (Reed Goodman, Collide dynacard thread).

**The email is a pointer. The attachment is the deliverable.** Cut roughly 85%.

What he kept, and what that implies:

- **No numbers, no tables, no reasoning in the body.** My draft explained the rod-string frequency, the load-datum anomaly and a rod-size comparison table. All of it went. The body says "See attached analysis `<filename>.pdf` with the well data available so far."
- **Name the attachment in the text** and **point at a section**: "Section 6 of the report is the way forward — three stages, and Stage A are probing questions."
- **Subject line is terse and lowercase**: `pump card — undulations`. No marketing phrasing.
- **One ask, one sentence.** "If you or other operators you know send the data. I will do the analysis and send it back — your wells, worked properly, at no charge."
- **Questions marked as questions**, including soft ones: "Any well survey or deviation information will also help?" and "Let me know?"
- **Personal context is included** when relevant (travel, availability) — it is not kept out as unprofessional.
- **Close**: `Thank you very much,` / `Vamsee` / phone `713-306-9029`.
- New thread with a fresh subject for a new topic is fine; the reply-into-existing-thread rule is not absolute (see [[feedback_vamsee_technical_outreach_email_style]]).

**Practical rule when drafting for him:** write the analysis into the PDF, then write an email that could be understood by someone who has not opened it yet — and stop. If a paragraph explains a finding, it belongs in the document.

He attaches PDFs himself by drag-drop, so producing the file and naming it in the body is enough; the Gmail tool's attachment path is not needed. See [[reference_gmail_create_draft_attachment_limit]].
