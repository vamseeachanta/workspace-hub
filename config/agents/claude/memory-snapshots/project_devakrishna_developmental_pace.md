devaKrishna (Vamsee's son, 10 chronologically, ~5 developmentally) has been learning singular vs. plural grammar for 5 years and is still working on it.

**Calibration implication:** For any skill goal (reading, typing, tennis, soccer, etc.), plan for **6–24 months per micro-milestone**, not weeks. Yearly progression tables with multi-step gains per year are miscalibrated.

**Motor/cardiovascular caveat:** Motor and aerobic capacity **outperform** language pace. Krishna treads water continuously for 1 hour (verified 2026-05-11) — well past survival-float level. Physical/procedural skills compound **faster** than symbolic/linguistic ones for him; swimming is the most accessible skill domain and a confidence/community platform (Special Olympics-tier competitive baseline).

**Operating frame: functional > academic.** Every goal answers "does this make daily life better or future independence easier?" rather than "does this match grade-level peers?"

**Trajectory implication for #73 (Achanta family long-term goals):** Strengthens **Plan-A** (lifetime support) trajectory signal — but doesn't preclude Plan-B per the asymmetric-cost rule. Plan-A scaffolding (Medicaid waitlist achantas-data#74, SNT in achantas-data#75) becomes even more time-critical.

**Why typing matters more than reading at this stage:** Typing bypasses speech-motor planning, becomes the expressive communication channel (potentially converging with AAC like Proloquo2Go). Reading is comprehension *of someone else's language*. For a child whose generalization is the bottleneck, expressive channels compound faster than receptive ones.

**How to apply:** When drafting devaKrishna-related goals, default to **activity menus** (concrete things to do during routine slots) over **goal hierarchies** (Y1→Y5 progression tables). Activity menus track engagement; goal hierarchies invite optimistic miscalibration. Canonical activity menu lives at `achantas-data/_family/krishna_activities.md` (tracking achantas-data#83).
