Floating Production, Storage and Offloading (FPSO) structural guides address both structural analysis (digital modeling) and operational/installation requirements (mooring, station-keeping, commissioning). Single FPSO documents must be indexed into both digitalmodel and mkt-a/lng-a repos to ensure discoverability across structural and operational disciplines.
