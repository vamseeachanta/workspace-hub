Features not listed in WRK acceptance criteria (e.g. --structure flag added to WRK-1056 quality script without being in the WRK body) emerge during plan/implementation, complicating review and scope boundary. Tight WRK-→-plan-→-code traceability is the gate. Plan reviewers should flag scope that is not anchored to the WRK ACs.
