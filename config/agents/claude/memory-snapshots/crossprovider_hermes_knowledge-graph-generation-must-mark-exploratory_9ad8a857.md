Knowledge graph generators produce both authoritative (graph-emitted) and exploratory/diagnostic (report-only) relationships. Confusion between the two can assign false authority to diagnostic findings. Explicitly label relationship types and ensure exploratory results remain in reports, not final graph edges, unless independently evidenced.
