Salesforce forbids AI scraping/training and content reuse but allows link+paraphrase with attribution; Telegram forbids contact lists and message content. Policies must be validated against official guidelines, not repo-drafted policy sketches, during plan reviews to prevent scope creep and legal misalignment.
