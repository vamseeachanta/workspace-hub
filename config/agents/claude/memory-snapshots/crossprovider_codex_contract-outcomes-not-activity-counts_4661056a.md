Specify deliverables by configurations, independently registered datasets, and QA criteria rather than by activity counts ('number of scans', 'passes', 'points'). Activity counts are hardware- and operator-dependent and do not guarantee coverage or quality. Establish pass rules: each critical surface ≥2 geometrically diverse views, deep occlusions ≥3.
