Review findings are incomplete without (a) explicit provider verdicts (APPROVE/MINOR/MAJOR), (b) specific file/section citations, and (c) durable evidence paths or issue/PR comment URLs. Empty reviews or reviews without citations are failures, not implicit approvals.
