Claude, Codex, and Gemini reviews of the same plan artifact surface different defect types. Claude catches governance gaps; Codex surfaces operational hazards; Gemini finds scope creep and productivity issues. Single-provider review misses ~33% of MAJOR-class findings. Use all three for critical designs.
