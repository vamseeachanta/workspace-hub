In oil & gas engineering, bot names signaling field credibility and operational reliability (e.g., 'RigIntel Bot', 'ACE Hermes Control Bot') outrank generic AI-personality names. Naming strategy should invoke reliability, offshore expertise, and production integrity rather than cuteness or trendy AI branding.
