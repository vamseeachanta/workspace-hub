Licensed-derived standards pages use frontmatter keys: visibility: private-llm-wiki, source_pdf (list of /mnt/ace/... paths), license_status (licensed-derived-data, encrypted-metadata-only). These distinguish licensed extraction from freely republishable content and flag that raw PDFs remain off-repo.
