Governance and policy documents (especially those defining approval, bypass, or enforcement logic) sustain meaningful review findings through many cycles (3–9 iterations observed). Findings focus on semantic precision (approval-after-revert edge cases, timestamp normalization contracts) rather than structural defects. Plan multiple review rounds for policy work.
