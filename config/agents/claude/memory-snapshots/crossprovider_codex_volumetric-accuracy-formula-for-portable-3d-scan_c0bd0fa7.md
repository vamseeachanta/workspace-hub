Volumetric accuracy over working distance follows A + B·L formula where A is local accuracy and B is drift per meter. For automotive scanning, target ≤0.05 mm local with ≤0.015 mm/m drift (~0.3 mm at 5 m). Distinguish accuracy (local closeness), volumetric accuracy (distance-dependent), resolution (sampling density), and range noise (sensor repeatability).
