Defects are classified MAJOR (blocking release) or MINOR (non-blocking). Every defect must cite exact file:line or section heading. Approval gating is binary: MAJOR count must equal zero. MINOR findings can exist in approved plans. This gate prevents advisory issues from silently accumulating and derailing release.
