Lumping drilling mud mass at the riser bottom ignores fluid compressibility; the mud column acts as an acoustic organ pipe with resonant frequencies that can coincide with vessel heave periods. For risers >2000 ft, this modeling choice produces non-conservative dynamic top tension predictions.
