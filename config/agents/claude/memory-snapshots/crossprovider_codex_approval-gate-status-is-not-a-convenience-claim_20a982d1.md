Plans cannot claim approval-readiness while acknowledging pending re-review requirements or known blockers. Approval status must be explicit and backed by clean re-review artifacts. Plans that say 'not ready until Codex/Gemini rerun is clean' cannot simultaneously claim readiness; they must wait for those reruns and be re-reviewed.
