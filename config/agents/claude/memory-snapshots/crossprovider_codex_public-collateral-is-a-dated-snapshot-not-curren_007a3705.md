Manufacturer datasheets, capability statements, and marketing material are historical baselines frozen at publication date. Absence from old documents ≠ current unavailability; equipment may be added, retired, rebuilt, or redeployed post-publication. Treat public material as a hypothesis anchor, not as ground truth.
