Technical standards from API, DNV, and similar vendors are vendor-licensed (marked 'Licensed by Information Handling Services'). Raw documents must not be committed to repositories; only derived data, citations, and extracted sections per established protocols (.claude/rules/codes-standards-data-routing.md) are permitted.
