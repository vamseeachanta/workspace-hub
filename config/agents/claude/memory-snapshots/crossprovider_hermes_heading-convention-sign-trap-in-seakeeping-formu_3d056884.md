In encounter frequency formula ω_e = ω - k·U·cos(β), setting β=0 gives FOLLOWING seas (ω_e decreases), and β=π gives HEAD seas (ω_e increases). This follows standard seakeeping convention (180° is head-on). Docstrings claiming 'β=0 = head seas' contradict the formula; always verify against the actual equation.
