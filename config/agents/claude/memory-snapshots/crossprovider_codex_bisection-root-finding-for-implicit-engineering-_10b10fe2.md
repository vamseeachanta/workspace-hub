For implicit equations without closed-form solutions but with bracketed root (e.g., DNV collapse pressure cubic), use bounded bisection rather than analytical methods. Robust, verifiable against specifications, and avoids numerical instabilities of Newton-Raphson in ill-conditioned domains.
