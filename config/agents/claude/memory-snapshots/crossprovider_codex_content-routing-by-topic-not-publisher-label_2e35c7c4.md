Publisher folder labels (DNV, SNAME, BSI) do not determine domain routing. A DNV PDF about metocean/hydro/mooring goes to marine-engineering, not engineering-standards. A drilling-equipment standard goes to drilling-engineering regardless of publisher. Route by actual content topic; note cross-domain dedupe hits during classification.
