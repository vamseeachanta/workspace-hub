Naval-architecture modules containing DNV, Whicker/Fehlner, IMO, or other standards-based formulas must comply with `.claude/rules/calc-citation-contract.md` and `docs/standards/calc-output-citation.md`. Every constant, coefficient, and formula source must be explicitly traceable and cited in inline comments. Assume zero background; cite edition/section numbers.
