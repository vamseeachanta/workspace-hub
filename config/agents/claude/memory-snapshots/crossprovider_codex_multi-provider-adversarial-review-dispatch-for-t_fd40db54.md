T3 complexity plans (large, cross-provider, or systemic) go through parallel adversarial reviews across Claude/Codex/Gemini providers with disagreement reconciliation. Reviews are stored in `scripts/review/results/` with per-provider and disagreement-summary artifacts. Prior-cycle blocker summaries are cited in plan metadata to avoid re-discovery.
