**Fact:** Krishna got stuck on a typing.com assignment 2026-05-12; typing.com's accuracy-gated lesson tree can stall a kid for weeks on a single tricky lesson.

**Rotation menu (all free, no signup required to start):**
- **TypingClub** (typingclub.com) — closest typing.com replacement, same K-12 lesson structure but lets you skip ahead without unlocking gates. Best first swap.
- **Dance Mat Typing** (bbc.co.uk/bitesize/topics/zf2f9j6) — BBC narrated story format, friendly animals, best for younger-developmental kids. Low-friction fun layer.
- **TypeTastic** (typetastic.com) — game-first, school-oriented, more visual than typing.com.
- **Nitro Type** / **Typeracer** — racing games, useful AFTER baseline typing is in place.
- **Keybr** (keybr.com) — adaptive drill, surfaces weak keys automatically. Dry but effective; reserve for once basics land.

**Why:** Per [[project_devakrishna_developmental_pace]], activity menus beat goal hierarchies for Krishna — rotating across 2-3 tools per week keeps engagement and means a stall in one doesn't stall the skill. Typing is a critical expressive channel (bypasses speech-motor planning), so velocity here matters more than lesson-tree completion.

**How to apply:**
- If Krishna stalls on any single typing platform, rotate before troubleshooting the assignment — switching is cheaper than unblocking.
- For "real" practice, 10 min/day of typing his own content (chat, journal, story) outperforms any structured lesson tree.
- Don't try to make him "finish" typing.com — it's a tool, not a curriculum target.
