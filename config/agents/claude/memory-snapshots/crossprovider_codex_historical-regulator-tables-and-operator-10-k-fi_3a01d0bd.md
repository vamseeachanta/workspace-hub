MITECO/CORES production tables publishing field output in both tonnes and barrels, plus operator Form 10-K net production rates, offer auditable conversion bases. Exact annual figures are stronger than rounded thousand-tonne tables. Search 1990s–2000s MITECO production-year PDFs and archived SEC filings.
