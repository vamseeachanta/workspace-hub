Distinguish accuracy (local closeness to truth) from volumetric error (spatial drift over distance, typically A + B·L formula), resolution (sampling density), and noise (repeatability). ISO 10360-13 specifies volumetric; headline 'accuracy' claims alone are misleading for multi-meter captures. At 5 m range, a 0.075 mm claimed accuracy with 0.010 mm/m volumetric spec yields actual ≈0.125 mm drift.
