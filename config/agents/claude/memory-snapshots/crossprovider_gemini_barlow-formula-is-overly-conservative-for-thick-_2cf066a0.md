The Barlow formula (Pd = 2kYt/D) relates maximum hoop stress to yield stress and is widely used in pipeline codes, but for thick-wall seamless pipe, actual burst strength significantly exceeds this prediction. This leads to unnecessarily thick-walled, expensive flowline designs with burst safety factors of 2–4 when 1.5–2.5 is typical for operating lines.
