Claude benefits from synthesis-heavy framing (challenge assumptions, surface tradeoffs, think hard); Codex from execution-heavy framing (precision, mechanical verification, minimize ambiguity); Gemini from exploration-heavy framing. This is non-obvious and produces materially different plan quality when applied intentionally rather than assuming all models respond to generic 'deep think' instructions.
