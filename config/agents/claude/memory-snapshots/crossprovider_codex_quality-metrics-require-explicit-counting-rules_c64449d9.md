Metrics like 'plan changes,' 'blockers prevented,' or self-reported 'confidence' are game-able and non-comparable across authors without upfront definitions of counting scope, attribution boundaries, and measurement conditions. Specify these in the metric schema; prefer outcome-oriented fields over speculative ones.
