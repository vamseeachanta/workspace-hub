Manufacturing/service cold calls respond better to "I've solved this problem 20 years and can automate the repetitive parts" than "here is a list of capabilities" or AI pitch. Name one specific repeated process you can eliminate, use proof links as secondary credibility (not the lead), and close with a low-pressure offer and one clear ask.
