Existing experimental hydrodynamic coefficients for bare and straked pipes lack coverage at Keulegan-Carpenter (KC) < 4 and reduced parameter (Beta) > 6000, a regime common in low-water-depth SCR fatigue analysis. Extrapolation in this gap produces drag coefficient uncertainty of several hundred percent, directly affecting riser fatigue life predictions.
