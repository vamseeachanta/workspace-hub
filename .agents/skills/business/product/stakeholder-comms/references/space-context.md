# Space context and transfer

Apply to project Spaces without importing another client's facts or commitments. Client-specific plans, source evidence and ongoing work belong in the owning private client repository and its authorized Space; common skills retain client-neutral methods only.

- Resolve the selected project's authoritative plan, discussions, decisions and ongoing work first. Verify ownership and audience before retrieval. Search membership is not permission for cross-client reuse; sanitized methods still require established rights, with restricted provenance retained privately.
- Reusable summaries are indexes to evidence. Carry locator/revision, source vintage, retrieval time, reporting as-of time and task-specific freshness criterion. Recheck material status against primary records; expose inaccessible sources and partial coverage. Incomplete search does not prove absence.
- Persistent Space retains discussions, decisions and ongoing work. Temporary `transferfilesonly` stages requested transfer files with a manifest identifying owning destination, revision, integrity evidence and disposition.
- A download button, local save or manifest is not a receipt. Read the saved artifact back; separately verify its intended durable destination. Never assume automatic download receipt, backup, recipient acceptance or publication.
- Before cleanup preserve unique work and link its verified retained destination. Remove only authorized redundant staging with verified copies. Unknown disposition blocks that cleanup. Transfers do not authorize removing persistent discussions/decisions/ongoing work.
