# Ship Stability

Hydrostatic stability of floating vessels — the ability to return to upright equilibrium after displacement.

## Key Concepts

- **Metacentric height (GM)** — vertical distance between center of gravity and metacenter
- **Righting arm (GZ)** — horizontal lever arm of restoring moment
- **GZ curves** — righting arm vs heel angle; primary stability assessment tool
- **Intact stability criteria** — IMO, SOLAS requirements for minimum stability
- **Damage stability** — stability after compartment flooding; SOLAS subdivision requirements
- **Free surface effect** — reduction in stability from partially filled tanks
