# GTM Energy Company Job Market Scan (Offshore Engineering)

## Market Demand Overview

-   **High Demand:** There is a strong and sustained demand for offshore engineering consultants, particularly those with experience in floating wind, subsea systems, and decommissioning.
-   **Key Hiring Companies:** Major operators (e.g., Shell, BP, Equinor), EPCI contractors (e.g., TechnipFMC, Subsea 7, Saipem), and specialized engineering consultancies (e.g., Wood, Aker Solutions, Worley) are actively hiring.
-   **Geographic Hotspots:** North Sea (UK, Norway), Gulf of Mexico (USA), Brazil, and Southeast Asia.

## Role Types & Required Skills

-   **Most Common Roles:**
    -   Senior/Lead Riser Engineer
    -   Mooring System Engineer
    -   Subsea Pipeline Engineer
    -   Floating Wind Installation Engineer
    -   Naval Architect (specializing in floating structures)
-   **Core Technical Skills in Demand:**
    -   **Software:** OrcaFlex, Flexcom, ANSYS Aqwa, Abaqus
    -   **Analysis:** Riser/mooring analysis, SURF (Subsea Umbilicals, Risers, and Flowlines) design, finite element analysis (FEA), hydrodynamics.
    -   **Standards:** API, DNV, ISO for offshore structures, risers, and pipelines.

## Compensation Trends

-   **Contract Roles:** Day rates for experienced consultants range from $800 to $1,500+, depending on experience, specialization, and location.
-   **Permanent Roles (Senior/Lead):** Base salaries typically range from $150,000 to $220,000, with significant bonus potential.
-   **Niche Skills Premium:** Expertise in floating wind, hydrogen, and carbon capture technologies commands a premium.
