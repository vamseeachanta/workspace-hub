# Field Development Plan (FDP) References

## Key Textbooks

1.  **"The Global Oil and Gas Industry: Management, Strategy and Finance"** by Andrew Inkpen and Michael H. Moffett - Provides a comprehensive overview of the business aspects of the oil and gas industry, essential for understanding the economic drivers of FDP.
2.  **"Petroleum Engineering: Principles and Practice"** by Carl Gatlin - Classic textbook covering the fundamentals of petroleum engineering, crucial for the technical basis of an FDP.
3.  **"Integrated Petroleum Reservoir Management"** by Abdus Satter and Ganesh C. Thakur - Focuses on a multidisciplinary approach to reservoir management, a core component of any FDP.

## Industry Standards and Recommended Practices (API)

-   **API RP 2A-WSD:** Recommended Practice for Planning, Designing, and Constructing Fixed Offshore Platforms—Working Stress Design.
-   **API RP 2T:** Recommended Practice for Planning, Designing, and Constructing Tension Leg Platforms.
-   **API RP 1111:** Design, Construction, Operation, and Maintenance of Offshore Hydrocarbon Pipelines (Limit State Design).

## Classification Societies Standards (DNV, ABS)

-   **DNV-OS-E301:** Position Mooring - Standard for mooring systems.
-   **ABS Rules for Building and Classing Mobile Offshore Drilling Units (MODUs)** - Comprehensive rules covering various aspects of offshore unit design and construction.

## Data Sources

-   **Wood Mackenzie:** Provides commercial intelligence and analysis for the energy, chemicals, metals and mining industries.
-   **IHS Markit:** Critical information, analytics, and solutions for the major industries and markets that drive economies worldwide.
-   **National Data Repositories (NDRs):** Government-managed databases of petroleum-related data (e.g., Norway's Diskos, UK's NSTA).
