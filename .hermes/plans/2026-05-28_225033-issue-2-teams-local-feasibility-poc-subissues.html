7. Non-negotiable gates before live Teams rollout

  • No broad Graph message-read scopes by default; do not request ChannelMessage.Read.All, Chat.Read, or tenant-wide history scopes for the POC unless separately approved.
  • Map Teams identity/channel context to repo content entitlements before returning restricted content.
  • Do not persist prompts, raw Teams bodies, retrieved snippets, model answers, or tokens by default.
  • Use read-only repo-scoped credentials or pinned knowledge packs; avoid personal PATs for runtime access.
  • All deliverables should remain HTML-first with provenance/manifest information.
