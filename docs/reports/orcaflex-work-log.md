## Update protocol for future agents

- Read this log, the runbook and handoff first; verify live issue/PR head and actual artifacts. Do not assume a historical pending or passed state still applies.
- After each important worked/did-not-work checkpoint, update the current position and append or amend its ledger row with revision, measured counts, evidence link, unresolved limit and existing owner. Preserve the original failed result and its repair disposition.
- Replace **pending** only after observing completed evidence at the relevant source head. Record current-head CI separately from local tests and private corpus replay. Never infer success from a running job, missing case, saved file, warning-only result or successful import.
- Keep denominators and stage names explicit; counts from overlapping suites are not additive. Record native calls and model scope accurately. No benchmark, registry or engineering-level promotion without its own evidence.
- Keep private paths, client names, raw issue bodies, credentials and host mappings out. Link sanitized tracked artifacts; retain private raw evidence under its existing preservation agreement.
