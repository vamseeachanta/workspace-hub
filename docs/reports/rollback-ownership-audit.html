Rollback ownership audit

Summary

The audit criterion is that a refused or unstarted action shall not acquire restoration authority from its presence in a batch snapshot. A started action may require recovery after a partial write. Issue 3868 defines this criterion and requires observation of untouched objects, rather than final bytes alone.

The isolated suite passes 13 cases. It confirms started-action filtering in the sibling repair caller and reproduces unresolved ownership gaps in the materializer and two backup-only shell helpers. Passing characterization cases establish the presence of those gaps; they do not qualify the helpers for concurrent use. Production helpers are unchanged. Repairs require their own bounded review.

Discovery uses text searches for rollback, restoration and recovery under scripts/, followed by inspection of the call sites listed below. The inventory is bounded: unrelated archival restore, memory synchronization, anti-rollback policy validation and ordinary branch cleanup are not transaction recovery and are excluded. A text search does not establish exhaustive coverage of arbitrary undo logic. No live recovery, installation or solver is executed.

Worked example: refusal between actions

Inputs: action A will replace a link whose original target is original; action B is initially absent. Both pass the initial batch validation. After A is changed, a concurrent writer creates B with bytes concurrent sentinel\n and native ownership becomes visible.

Method: the second validation refuses B. The independent expected restoration set is {A}, because only A passed its last validation and entered mutation. A is restored to its original target. B retains its bytes, device, inode, mode and modification/change times. A spy records the exact restore set and path removals, detecting removal/recreation even if the final bytes match.

Answer: all file, symlink and directory sentinel variants retain B, with only A passed to restoration. The partial-write variant fails after unlinking A and still restores A. The unsafe negative control passes the whole snapshot directly to restoration and deletes B, demonstrating that the fixture detects the defect.

Worked example: intent is not started mutation

Inputs: a materializer entry has absent preimage and planned bytes postimage\n. A staged payload exists. Method: after replace-intent is saved, the injected concurrent writer creates the destination with those same planned bytes. The real replacement helper refuses because the destination no longer matches the absent preimage.

Answer: the staged payload remains untouched, yet rollback_candidates selects the foreign destination because intent and postimage digest match. This is an unresolved ownership defect, not a successful recovery. A repair must distinguish refused replacement from a replacement that began, while retaining recovery for an actual partial mutation.
