#3347 — Converge setup-cron on the transactional installer

Status: draft · Complexity: T2 · Lane: Codex

Live read-only reproduction: legacy setup lists 56 desired entries without comparing live ownership. Transactional preview fails closed on two duplicate Deckhand presence-sync lines. The live crontab also contains duplicate notification purge lines; equality refresh was manually repaired at its current six-hour cadence.

Proposed outcome

setup-cron.sh will become a compatibility wrapper over cron_apply.py. Linux installs will use one fail-closed transaction with explicit ownership, compare-and-swap, backup, preservation checks, and rollback.
