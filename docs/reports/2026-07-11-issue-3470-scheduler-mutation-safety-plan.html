Scheduler Mutation Safety Contract

Status: plan-review · Complexity: T3 · Implementation blocked pending user approval.

Outcome

A fail-closed registry and automatic enforcement gate will cover eight direct scheduler owners and three transitive entrypoints without changing live scheduler behavior.

Core contract

  • Targets belong to operations, allowing one owner to represent systemd-user and current-user cron safely.
  • Every destructive authority branch will be enumerated and source-attested by closed checker-owned evaluators.
  • Substring ownership remains migration-required; it cannot be certified compliant.
  • Index evidence will use NUL-safe Git plumbing and a versioned, length-framed raw-byte digest.
  • The PR workflow will parse as an active failure-propagating gate, not an advisory invocation.

Adversarial review

Codex r3: APPROVE. Focused governance r4: APPROVE. Focused scheduler/provenance r4: APPROVE. Claude and Gemini were unavailable and are recorded as such.

Safety boundary

No crontab, systemd unit, Windows scheduled task, or process will be mutated by planning or verification. Behavioral migrations will require separate linked issues and approval gates.
