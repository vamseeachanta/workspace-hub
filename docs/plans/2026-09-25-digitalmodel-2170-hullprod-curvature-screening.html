What was evaluated

HullProd 1.0.1 (Serani and Maki, CNR-INM, BSD-3-Clause, pip install hullprod, arXiv:2609.27544) computes a seven-value curvature signature from a hull surface: I_D (area-averaged |K| L_ref^2), its elliptic and saddle parts, and area fractions flat / single / elliptic / saddle. It also exports per-vertex Gaussian and mean curvature, curvature-class maps and mesh-quality diagnostics. It was run on seven ecosystem hulls and three analytical controls on 2026-09-25. Nothing in the ecosystem computes surface curvature as a quality measure today; HullParametricSpace can only scale a catalog hull in L, B, T and D.

Execution method and limits

  1. Each approved row will receive its own dated plan under digitalmodel/docs/plans/ and its own issue mapped to 2170, with RED tests before implementation. No code will change under this page alone.
  2. Every signature will be computed with an explicit lref equal to length_bp and recorded with the panel count, HullProd version and input hash from provenance.json. Automatic reference length will not be used for catalog records.
  3. Comparisons between hulls will use matched panel density or the BRep backend. A single coarse BEM mesh under-reads I_D by 10 to 15 %; that bias will be stated wherever a value is quoted.
  4. Client hulls (client drillship DS-A, client heavy-lift vessel HLV-A, FPSO) will remain in the private client wiki sibling cases. Their signatures may be quoted in public repositories only as dimensionless values without geometry, as in Table 1.
  5. The HullProd thresholds h_f = k_f = 1e-4 are numerical classification thresholds. No plate-forming or fabrication-cost claim will be derived from them.
  6. Existing RAO and hydrostatics regressions touched by D3 will be re-baselined only with a before and after signature table in the plan evidence.

Acceptance and risks

Acceptance for this page will be a filled decision register. Acceptance for each approved row will be defined in its own plan, but at minimum: D1 will reproduce the three analytical controls to three decimals; D2 will show a sweep where scaled variants share one signature and a form change does not; D3 will show elliptic bow and stern on the fixture ship and an unchanged Wigley signature; D4 will reject the Sea Cypress OBJ and pass every GDF in Table 1.

Risks: HullProd is a 1.0.1 release from one group; pin the version and keep the spike scripts as an independent check. The OpenCascade wheel adds about 150 MB to any environment that installs the extra; keep it optional. D3 changes generated geometry and will move existing RAO baselines; without the before and after signature table this will look like a regression. Client hull geometry must not leave the private client wiki sibling.
