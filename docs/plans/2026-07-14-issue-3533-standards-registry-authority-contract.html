Adversarially reviewed draft · Issue #3533

Standards facts must remain separate.

The registry will stop treating publisher currentness, a filename, transfer completion, lawful access, permission, and technical acceptance as interchangeable facts.

Public-safe

What workspace-hub will hold

  • Canonical code identity
  • Official publisher designation and evidence date
  • Implementation-basis metadata
  • Transfer history with verified or legacy-unverified evidence
  • A blocked or metadata-only resolution gate

What it will not publish

  • Document fingerprints or exact holdings
  • Private paths or access evidence
  • Permission or licensing decisions
  • Qualified-review details
  • Licensed text, tables, OCR, or coefficients

Private authority evidence requires separately approved llm-wiki issue #840.

Four independent questions

QuestionEvidenceFail-closed rule
What is current?Official publisher catalog and verification timeDoes not prove possession or applicability
What was transferred?Destination alias plus reconciled content identityLegacy empty-path done becomes legacy-unverified
What may be used?Private, purpose-scoped access/permission evidencePossession and subscription imply nothing
What is technically accepted?Qualified decision against an exact source identityCurrent reachability affects usability, not historical decision integrity

Implementation controls

  • Closed JSON/YAML schema and strict enums
  • Logical joins by code ID; holding joins by exact namespaced content identity
  • Injected build time, deterministic dry run, validation, atomic replacement
  • Consumer migration across reports, lookup, readiness, research, and domain views
  • Hermetic digest, chronology, collision, and disclosure tests

Human gates

  • This plan requires explicit user approval after review.
  • Private evidence storage requires a separate approved plan.
  • A qualified reviewer must decide any technical basis.
  • No approval here authorizes DNV numerical changes or licensed-source inspection.

Delivery sequence

#3533 public schema and conservative records → private authority contract (#840) plus citation/page identity contracts (#3538/#839) → blocked resolver mechanics (#837) → qualified C203 basis decision (#1588) → exact activation and calculation implementation.
