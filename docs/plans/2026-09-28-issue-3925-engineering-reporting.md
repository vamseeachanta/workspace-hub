## Proposed Work and Pseudocode

1. The review will inventory available source/session sets and current claims; unavailable histories will remain explicit gaps.
2. The standard will map each new convention to the applicable source and resolve older presentation conflicts.
3. Claude, Codex and available Agy/Gemini review lanes will hunt defects in the exact proposal. Unavailable providers will be recorded; malformed review output will block acceptance.
4. The owner will review the resulting standard and exact adoption scope before shared instructions change.
5. After approval, one authority will be published through an isolated branch; existing guides and skills will receive narrow pointers or supersession notes. Generated runtimes will be rebuilt through the existing script, never edited by hand.
6. Each enumerated machine will receive the reviewed revision through existing authorized sync/install mechanisms only when its checkout and runtime targets are verified and no active work will be overwritten. Conflicting/dirty targets will wait with a named gap.
7. Existing live-session messaging will submit the revision and concise conventions to each addressable active session, preserving delivery receipts and distinguishing queued from acknowledged/adopted. Unaddressable providers will receive a durable handoff only where a supported channel exists; a file drop will not count as live delivery.
8. Final checks will verify source/artifact readback, hashes, repository state, review results and exact fleet coverage. The task will remain open for unreachable or unverified targets.

## Files to Change and Boundaries

Only the paths in the artifact map will be candidates for approved implementation. Additional skill pointers will be enumerated before editing. Repo-local AGENTS files will not be bulk rewritten. No model, calculation, tolerance, result dataset, solver, authentication, hook or fleet topology will change. Existing client reports will not be regenerated or reissued under this proposal. Published history and archived evidence will not be deleted. Machine-specific credentials and raw session transcripts will not be copied into public Git.

## Acceptance Criteria

The standard will reflect all accessible reviewed comments with explicit coverage gaps. The exact published revision will match the reviewed artifact. Shared pointers and each claimed machine installation will resolve to that revision. Every addressable active session in the observed roster will have a delivery result; outstanding acknowledgements, inaccessible machines and unsupported channels will remain open. The cleanup audit will name preserved unrelated state and task artifacts.

## Adversarial Review Summary

Reviews will be recorded against the proposal digest. Blocking findings will be resolved before adoption approval is requested; a review will not itself grant approval.

## Risks and Open Questions

The latest formal report sequence will conflict with older fixed renderer schemas; this change will update conventions without claiming renderer compliance. Remote history may be inaccessible, stale or incomplete. A session roster may change during delivery; observation times will bound the claim. Local bus messaging may not support every provider. Existing active report authors may own the target files; claims and narrow writes will preserve that work. The source's requirement to remove superseded current results will not authorize deletion of archived evidence.
