## §1 — Tier 1 Priority Sequence

Ordered list of P1 targets and *why first* — these are the highest-leverage outreach
slots based on (a) demo-fit density, (b) public fleet evidence quality, and (c)
campaign-narrative alignment with `docs/gtm/email-outreach-templates.md`.

1. **Subsea7 (#1)** — vessel-canonical match: our `seven-borealis.yaml` intake
   already proxies the Seven Borealis class, giving the most credible
   vessel-specific re-run conversation. Demo 3 + Demo 5 land directly.
2. **Heerema (#6)** — Sleipnir/Thialf are the textbook splash-zone-slamming
   prospects for Demo 3; the "200te+ where DAF alone is misleading" angle
   from `capability-map.md` is dead-center for their fleet.
3. **Allseas (#5)** — Pioneering Spirit is so distinctive that any screening
   conversation gets engineering-leadership attention; Demo 4 also covers their
   Solitaire/Audacia shallow-water S-lay portfolio.
4. **Edison Chouest (#16)** — top GoM operator with MPSV capability; the
   GoM-niche evidence lane (#2562) makes them P1 once mapped to D3/D5.
5. **Hornbeck (#15)** — second GoM P1; HOS Achiever / HOS Iron Horse are
   public-evidence MPSVs that map cleanly to Demo 3 mudmat and Demo 5 jumper.
6. **TechnipFMC (#2)** — Deep Blue + Deep Energy cover both flex/rigid pipelay
   and subsea tiebacks; Demo 5 jumper installation has the highest fit.
7. **Saipem (#3)** — Castorone/S7000 cover both tier-1 demos; outreach should
   lead with Demo 4 catenary-screening because S-lay is their public hook.
8. **McDermott (#4)** — GoM deepwater pipelay overlap with our portfolio
   narrative; Demo 2 multi-code reconciliation is the clean entry.

**Sequencing logic.** Send the first wave to (1)–(4) before the rest because
those are the ones where a senior engineer at the prospect can immediately
pattern-match the demo to a real candidate field they've worked. (5)–(8) follow
within the same week with subject-line variants from the email templates.
