The actual reason Windows tasks are excluded today is incidental: `provider-dream-bridge-win`/`hermes-claude-bridge-win` are pinned `machines:[ace-win-1,ace-win-2]` (verified lines 398, 437), so on `dev-primary` they hit the `legacy_excludes` conflict branch (`role-match` control-plane + machine exclusion → `roles_authoritative` unset → not selected, **conflict reported**).
