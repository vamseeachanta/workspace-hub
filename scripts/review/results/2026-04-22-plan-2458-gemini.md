### Issues Found

- [MINOR] Scope vs. #2462 registry — the plan adds an entry to `digitalmodel/src/digitalmodel/benchmarks/inventory.py` while flagging that #2462 may reshape `specs/module-registry.yaml`. The stated mitigation is "declared as open item, not a blocker" and a follow-up reconciliation. Sharper: pin an explicit schema contract ("if #2462 lands a registry with `benchmark_id`-keyed entries, the inventory.py entry is the source of truth and #2462 must adapter-read from it, OR the inventory.py entry must be reverted and re-expressed in the new registry") so the two issues cannot silently drift into schema incompatibility. Today the plan acknowledges the risk without binding a resolution shape.

- [MINOR] Sibling-parallelism surface overlap with #2457 — Files to Change modifies both `digitalmodel/docs/domains/orcawave/README.md` and `docs/roadmaps/orcawave-orcaflex-canonical-spec-contract-roadmap.md`. Risk mitigation says "targets distinct subsections … distinct roadmap lines (120 region)". The roadmap file is a single one-page doc, and #2457's sibling promotion will almost certainly also annotate it (single-body benchmark bullet lives near the multi-body bullet). Recommend: name the exact line/anchor per sibling (e.g., "#2458 only touches the multi-body bullet at line 120; #2457 owns its own bullet") or defer the roadmap edit to whichever sibling lands second via a follow-on merge commit.

- [MINOR] "Bridge-candidate readiness" phrasing risk — manifest field `bridge_candidates: ["future OrcaWave -> OrcaFlex handoff validation (extends closed #1605/#1592/#1768 with a multi-body case)"]`. Closed #1605/#1592/#1768 are described in the plan as "delivered foundations". A future reader parsing the manifest could infer that the multi-body case is already tested against those closed foundations, rather than understanding it as "structurally ready to be added to a future handoff test". Recommend tightening the manifest string to "candidate for future OrcaWave -> OrcaFlex handoff test expansion; not exercised end-to-end against closed #1605/#1592/#1768 by #2458" — the plan's risk table states this correctly, but the manifest text is the user-facing artifact.

- [MINOR] Claim-boundary wording drift — roadmap line 8 says "near-equivalent for key engineering inputs and tested pathways, not strict identity across every native YAML field". The plan's manifest `claim_boundary` paraphrases as "near-equivalent for key engineering inputs per canonical-spec roadmap; not strict identity across every native YAML field". The "and tested pathways" clause is dropped. Minor, but since this boundary is load-bearing, prefer verbatim quotation from the roadmap.
