## Findings
1. The plan hard-blocks approval on provider unavailability, which contradicts the repo’s review policy. `docs/standards/AI_REVIEW_ROUTING_POLICY.md` says unavailable reviewers are allowed: “Continue with remaining agents, but record the missing reviewer.” This plan instead says in `Acceptance Criteria` and `Adversarial Review Summary` that the gate stays blocked on `UNAVAILABLE` artifacts unless the user explicitly authorizes reduced-provider review. That is a policy change, not a plan detail, and it is unsupported by the cited standard.
2. The recurring weekly audit is being coupled to issue-specific state. In `Pseudocode`, the plan requires `config/skills/filesystem-only-skill-dispositions.yaml` to own a top-level `issue_body_paths` list seeded with the six `#2488` paths and requires recurring output field `issue_body_six_drift`. Current recurring surfaces are generic: `scripts/skills/weekly_skills_audit.py`, `config/skills/weekly-audit-policy.yaml`, and `docs/ops/scheduled-tasks.md` describe a repo-wide weekly audit, not an issue-bound tracker. Embedding `#2488` path memory into the permanent cron contract is unverified scope expansion.
3. The plan introduces mandatory provenance/guard infrastructure that is not justified by issue `#2488` or any cited current contract. Issue `#2488` asks for deterministic inventory, classification/disposition of filesystem-only skills, and weekly reporting. This plan additionally makes implementation depend on `tests/fixtures/skills/issue-2488-archived-duplicate-baseline.json`, `scripts/skills/verify_issue_2488_baseline_order.sh`, `scripts/skills/verify_issue_2488_baseline_order.py`, branch-range ancestry checks, and strict closeout gating around `baseline_source_commit`. None of those surfaces exist today, and none are mentioned in `#2488`, `#2486`, `docs/ops/scheduled-tasks.md`, or `docs/standards/weekly-skills-audit-policy.md`. This is major critical-path inflation.
4. The scanner/tooling contract is asserted without evidence that the tools exist or are part of repo policy. In `validate_and_render_dispositions_for_manual_reconcile`, the plan requires scan attestation with `tool_or_method` in `gitleaks|trufflehog|manual:full-file-secret-scan`. Repo search returned no matches for `gitleaks` or `trufflehog`, and none of the cited standards/docs establish them as available or required. A plan cannot hard-require unverified tooling.
5. The tentative disposition evidence uses stale `.gitignore` citations. The plan claims `.gitignore:270 personal-*` and `.gitignore:291 memory/` in the `Tentative disposition table`, but the current `.gitignore` content places `personal-*` and `memory/` elsewhere. Those citations are part of the plan’s evidence chain for why certain skills are ignored; stale line references make the evidence non-verifiable. `.gitignore` does contain `digitalmodel/`, `personal-*`, and `memory/`, but not at the cited locations.
