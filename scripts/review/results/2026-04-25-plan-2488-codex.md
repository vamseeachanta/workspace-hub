## Findings
1. The plan suppresses unresolved active loss-risk after “review” without requiring terminal reconciliation. In `Pseudocode -> validate_and_render_dispositions`, it says `reviewed_file_sha256 matching applies only to non-terminal unchanged filesystem-only entries`, and the TDD list includes `test_disposition_ledger_suppresses_previously_reviewed_matching_paths`. That masks still-untracked active skills on later weekly runs even though the deliverable says the workflow should “surface active filesystem-only skills as loss-risk findings” and issue `#2488` defines them as loss-risk until committed/archived/ignored/deleted. Reviewed-but-still-active is not resolved.

2. The `_diverged` rule is internally inconsistent and will produce contradictory implementation/tests. `Pseudocode -> build_skill_inventory_with_git` says loss-risk filtering should treat only `_archive` and `_archived` as non-active and “report legacy `_diverged` exclusion separately,” but the TDD list includes `test_inventory_summary_excludes_archive_archived_and_diverged_from_loss_risk`, and Acceptance Criteria says “`_diverged` is reported as a separate legacy-excluded compatibility count” while also requiring `_diverged` to be excluded from active loss-risk. Issue `#2488` only says active counts exclude `_archive` and `_archived`. The plan needs one definition.
