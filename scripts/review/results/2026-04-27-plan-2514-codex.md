    29|Impacted plan section: Scope lines 154-155; Pseudocode lines 172-215; Tests lines 261-275; Acceptance Criteria line 284
    30|Why it matters: Radial layer continuity/order cannot be validated safely if layers use mixed units or arbitrary unit strings.
