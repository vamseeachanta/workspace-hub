### Suggestions
- Replace the step-2 assertion with a check that evaluates effective rule enablement, not just `v is False`. At minimum, fail if the config sets `default: false` without explicitly enabling every floor rule, and fail if any floor rule is absent under such a config shape.
- Remove the host-exclusion language from `## Risks and Open Questions` and keep one policy everywhere: per-URL excludes only, with measurable enforcement steps for the `<=5% of unique URLs` threshold.
- Fix the TDD section so tests/validators are defined before authoring the artifacts, or stop claiming AGENTS-compliant TDD for the workflow/config steps. Also cite `AGENTS.md` by section (`Hard Gates`, item 2) instead of an unverified line number.
- Reconsider the `validate-workflows` deferral. If it stays deferred, the plan should explicitly acknowledge that workflow integrity is only locally checked and that this remains an accepted residual risk rather than calling the CI restoration complete.
