## Findings

### MAJOR

1. **Three of five TDD classification exemplars cite files that do not exist.** Plan lines 133, 134, 135 name:
   - `knowledge/wikis/marine-engineering/wiki/concepts/catenary.md` — NOT PRESENT. `ls marine-engineering/wiki/concepts/` returns only `cathodic-protection-system.md` and `mooring-line-failure.md`. There is no `catenary.md`.
   - `knowledge/wikis/engineering/wiki/concepts/added-mass.md` — NOT PRESENT. `ls engineering/wiki/concepts/` shows `hydrodynamic-analysis.md` and `cfd-offshore-hydrodynamics.md`; no `added-mass.md`.
   - `knowledge/wikis/marine-engineering/wiki/standards/api-rp-2sk.md` — NOT PRESENT. The `marine-engineering/wiki/` tree has no `standards/` subdirectory at all (`find -maxdepth 2 -type d` returns only `comparisons, concepts, entities, sources, visualizations`). The only standards subtree under wikis lives at `engineering/wiki/standards/` and holds `api-579-ffs.md, dnv-os-e301.md, dnv-rp-c203.md, dnv-rp-c205.md, dnv-rp-f101.md, dnv-rp-f105.md, ocimf-meg4.md` — no `api-rp-2sk.md`. Acceptance criterion (line 147) depends on reviewers applying the doc to these exact pages. Fabricated exemplars invalidate the entire acceptance gate. Plan must pick real paths before approval.

2. **Deny-list vendor-scope is catastrophically under-specified.** Plan line 96 names only "Orcina, AQWA, BEMRosetta." `grep "^| collection" knowledge/wikis/marine-engineering/wiki/sources/` returns 30 unique licensed/third-party collections actively ingested into the wiki: **SPE, OMAE, OTC, ISOPE, NACE, SNAME, API, ISO 9001, IADC, SUT, JPT, DOT, EUCI, Euroforum, IMarEST, Pipeline Pigging & Integrity Management, Rio Oil & Gas, Subsea Tieback, Arctic Technology, Coiled Tubing, DeepGulf, Dry Tree Forum, Flow Induced Vibration, Offshore West Africa, Unlocking Deepwater Potential Mumbai**, plus the OMAE2009-79769 paper. Every one of those collections is copyright-controlled conference/standards-body material; redistributing derivatives to aceengineer.com for SEO carries identical license risk as Orcina manual derivatives — arguably greater, since SPE/API pursue redistribution more actively. The plan's prose framing "vendor-derivative material is deny-listed by default" (line 171) is contradicted by the allow-list line 90, which enables "standards-referenced methodology explainers (general public domain or fair-use material)." A reviewer cannot deterministically apply the list when 30 publisher collections are unnamed and the defaulting rule conflicts with the enumerated allow-list.

3. **Memory-content characterization is incomplete and the plan ignores a memory-derived hard rule.** Plan line 33 cites `project_aceengineer_copy_canonical_sources.md` for "llm-wiki OUT OF SCOPE for firm copy." The actual memory file goes further: item 3 says llm-wiki is "explicitly OUT OF SCOPE for firm copy per `engineering/wiki/overview.md` ('how we engineer, not what we engineer'). Only consult for domain/technical claims." Crucially, the memory also encodes a **"Hard rule discovered"** (live-site negatives are canonical — e.g. `about.html`'s "We deliver automated workflows, not consulting hours" and "AceEngineer is the firm ... not a contractor with a laptop"). The plan's allow-list (line 87–91) permits "anonymized technical case-study narratives" and "open-source tool capability explanations" to flow to GTM with no requirement to vet against live-site negative constraints. That re-opens the drift path the memory explicitly closed. Boundary doc must reference live-site-negatives as an additional filter stage, or the sanitization contract is weaker than the memory it claims to codify.

4. **Live wiki page `engineering/wiki/concepts/knowledge-to-website-pipeline.md` actively promotes the #2022 pipeline and is not addressed.** Plan proposes re-scoping #2022 but leaves an in-tree, indexed wiki page (frontmatter `tags: [gtm, website, publishing, content-pipeline, aceengineer]`, added 2026-04-08) that narrates "Turn repo knowledge bases into client-facing aceengineer.com content" in 4 phases, identical to the #2022 body. The page even cites "19K+" wiki pages for auto-publish. If the boundary doc lands and this page stays, agents following wiki guidance will still pursue the forbidden pipeline. The Files-to-Change table (lines 115–121) must include either retraction, superseded-by header, or reconciliation edit to this page. Otherwise the decision is cosmetic.

5. **Boundary scope excludes three high-leakage surfaces where GTM-relevant material actually lives.** Plan line 22 enumerates allow-list candidate surfaces only within `knowledge/wikis/`. Adjacent but in-repo surfaces:
   - `knowledge/dark-intelligence/` (xlsx-poc, geotechnical, xlsx-poc-v2) — contains the "Dark Intelligence Excel" learnings the user has explicitly flagged (feedback_dark_intelligence_excel). High client-competitive sensitivity; never named in the plan.
   - `knowledge/seeds/` — `maritime-law-cases.yaml`, `mooring-failures-lng-terminals.yaml`, `career-learnings.yaml`. Case-study anonymization hinges on these seeds; plan's deny-list does not mention them.
   - `.planning/research/2026-04-02-competitor-market.md` — explicit competitor content; if any wiki page is derived from it downstream, the allow-list cannot detect that lineage.
   - `data/document-index/conference-*` — registry/manifests referencing licensed conference papers; a doc-index → wiki → GTM chain is the primary leakage path not just wiki → GTM.
   The plan title is "llm-wiki → GTM boundary" but the real problem surface is "repo knowledge → GTM boundary." Either rename/rescope, or explicitly out-of-scope these surfaces with rationale. Currently they fall through silently.

### MINOR

1. **Sanitization contract fails the borderline test.** Plan lines 100–104 use prose verbs ("strip client names", "generalize numeric examples", "add attribution footer"). Apply to `knowledge/wikis/engineering/wiki/concepts/mooring-line-failure-physics.md`: is the mere fact "Prelude FLNG suffered mooring-line failure" (public-record incident, extensively reported) a "client identifier" requiring strip? Reviewer A says public-record → allow. Reviewer B says any named vessel/operator → strip. Plan offers no tie-breaker. Plan line 138 even includes a "Prelude FLNG mooring" entity in the wiki (`entities/prelude-flng-mooring.md`) which exemplifies this ambiguity. Add: "named incidents must appear in ≥2 public sources (regulator report, peer-reviewed paper, or news of record)" or similar mechanical test.

2. **Acceptance criterion "classification exercises pass (reviewer judgment matches doc)" has no mechanical alternative proposed even though one exists.** The engineering wiki already mandates YAML frontmatter (SCHEMA in `knowledge/wikis/engineering/CLAUDE.md`: `title, tags, added, last_updated, sources`). A concrete mechanical gate: add a frontmatter field `gtm_classification: allow|deny|review` that each wiki page must carry, plus a linter (`scripts/enforcement/check-wiki-gtm-classification.sh`) that fails CI if any page lacks it or if a deny-classified page is referenced by an allow-list export script. That is Level-2 enforcement (per `.claude/rules/patterns.md`). Prose-exercise acceptance is Level-0 and contradicts the project's own enforcement-gradient guidance. Plan line 7 lists complexity T2; mechanical gate does not raise it.

3. **Plan lines 23–26 "5 domain wikis" and "5 pages" for personal are stale.** `ls knowledge/wikis/` shows SIX domain wikis (engineering, marine-engineering, maritime-law, naval-architecture, personal, health-reports) plus the top-level `cross-links.md`. Plan silently omits `health-reports/` (which holds `health-2026-04-07.json/.md` — by far the most sensitive category, PII-grade, no mention of deny-listing). Also `personal/wiki/` currently has exactly 5 markdown files but the count is descriptive, not prescriptive, and including `health-reports` makes the wiki population clearly different from plan's framing.

4. **`#2022` re-scope recommendation is not empty but is near-null.** Issue #2022 acceptance criteria: "10+ wiki domains published", "5+ engineering skills converted to service pages", "daily cron auto-publish", "contact form with CRM". Plan re-scope = "methodology/educational allow-list content only." Of #2022's four acceptance criteria, three (domain publishing, skills→service, daily cron) are now effectively forbidden under the boundary (skills → service pages carries firm-positioning deny-list; cron auto-publish is incompatible with mandatory manual review at line 104). Only "educational methodology pages via manual review" survives. This is closer to closing than re-scoping. Plan should either (a) be honest and recommend close-with-rationale, or (b) specify a 3–5 page pilot surface so "re-scope" has a measurable target, otherwise #2022 becomes perpetually open with no completion path.

## Verdict rationale

Five MAJOR defects: three fabricated TDD exemplars break the acceptance gate; the deny-list misses 30 licensed publisher collections actually ingested today; plan under-represents a hard live-site-negatives rule already captured in memory; the wiki itself contains a live page (`knowledge-to-website-pipeline.md`) promoting the forbidden pipeline with no reconciliation action; and three adjacent surfaces (`dark-intelligence/`, `seeds/`, `health-reports/`) that carry equal or greater GTM-leakage risk fall outside the plan's scope silently. Four MINORs compound. This is a MAJOR. Plan should revise file citations, expand deny-list to enumerate publisher collections (or cite a mechanical publisher-field linter), add the engineering wiki page to the Files-to-Change list, promote acceptance to a frontmatter-field mechanical check, and either broaden scope or explicitly out-of-scope adjacent knowledge surfaces before re-review.
