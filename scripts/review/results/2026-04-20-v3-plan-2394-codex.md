### Verdict: MAJOR

### Summary
The dominant defect class is unverified correctness-critical assertions. The plan embeds claims about issue state, file existence, operating-model carve-outs, and template updates, but it does not provide verifiable evidence sufficient to affirm the required dependency, identity, tiering, or test-coverage claims.
