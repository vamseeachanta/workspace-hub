## FINDINGS

1. **BLOCKER — D5's `legacy:no-trigger` fallback silently swallows every second-cycle non-reactivation transition, including the grace sweep's own purges.** D5 scopes the timestamp fallback to reactivation edges only (`awaiting-reply|completed|purged -> extracted|inbound`); all other id-less repeats get the constant `dedup_event_id = "legacy:no-trigger"`, which by design dedups across fresh timestamps. Walk a legitimate multi-cycle thread: `completed -> purged` (sweep, no message id) … reply reactivates to `extracted` … `extracted -> completed` … second sweep's `completed -> purged` now has dedup key `(account, thread, completed, purged, legacy:no-trigger)` — identical to cycle one — and is dropped. The snapshot is stuck at `completed` forever and `sweep_grace()` re-plans and re-swallows the purge on every run. Same defect hits the second `inbound -> extracted` after a missing-extraction reactivation (D8 sends the thread to `inbound`; its re-extraction dedups against cycle one's `inbound -> extracted`), and second-cycle `extracted -> completed` / `extracted -> awaiting-reply` whenever the caller lacks a message id. D9 gives `sweep_grace` no way to mint a per-cycle dedup id, and the TDD list has no multi-cycle test (`test_sweep_apply_rebuilds_snapshot_meta` uses one eligible record, one cycle). This is the residual form of Gemini r2 finding #5 — the patch fixed only the reactivation edges the finding happened to name, not the defect class.
