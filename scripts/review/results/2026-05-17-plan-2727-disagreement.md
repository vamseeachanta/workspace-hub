- The plan fails the issue-class retrieval contract for a labeled data/knowledge issue. Issue [#2727](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/workspace-hub/issues/2727) is labeled `cat:data-pipeline`, `cat:document-intelligence`, and `domain:knowledge`. `docs/plans/README.md` requires Data Pipeline plans to consult `registry.yaml`, pipeline config, and `resource-intelligence-maturity.yaml`, and Knowledge/Intelligence plans to consult the operating model, sibling contracts, accessibility map, and accessibility registry where available. The plan’s “Documents consulted” omits `data/document-index/registry.yaml`, `data/document-index/resource-intelligence-maturity.yaml`, `docs/document-intelligence/README.md`, and the normative document-intelligence docs listed there.
- The plan omits issue [#2727](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/workspace-hub/issues/2727)’s own historical grounding source. The issue body explicitly cites `docs/BUSINESS_BRAIN.md` as defining promotion through source, provenance, license, and legal sanity gates. The plan’s “Documents consulted” does not include `docs/BUSINESS_BRAIN.md`, even though that file contains the “Knowledge promotion” and “Legal Sanity Gates for Public Artifacts” rules directly relevant to the proposed promotion model.

The plan’s “Files to Change” creates `docs/architecture/data-source-inventory.md`, while “Client data handling model” lists raw paths such as `/mnt/ace/rock-oil-field`, `/mnt/ace/doris`, `/mnt/ace/acma-projects`, and `/mnt/ace/saipem`. `docs/BUSINESS_BRAIN.md` identifies `workspace-hub` as public, and its legal gate says no confidential/client-identifying content may be promoted.
