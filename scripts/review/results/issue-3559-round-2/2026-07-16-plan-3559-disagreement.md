### codex

- The acceptance transaction is not atomic. Plan lines 216–220 say it will “atomically append accepted event and replace accepted-release pointer,” but those are separate files at lines 121–122 and 234–235. A crash can leave an accepted ledger event with the old pointer or the new pointer without its event. `test_accepted_pointer_updates_atomically_after_authorized_smoke` at line 267 cannot make a two-file operation atomic. The plan needs one authoritative atomic object or a write-ahead/recovery protocol with crash-point tests.
- The concurrency model cannot serialize the declared topology. `record_candidate` uses a “repository-local lock” and local `expected_accepted_hash` at lines 211–214, while line 10 declares `parallel-worktree`. Separate worktrees or machines do not share that lock or working-copy state. No remote-ref CAS, fetch-before-write, push-race handling, or recovery rule exists.
