**Why this matters:** A 50-source bar may be either too low (Stanford OCW alone offers >50 lectures) or arbitrarily high (10 high-quality sources may serve Kaggle better than 50 mediocre ones).

**Recommendation:** Reframe as "≥30 high-quality (textbook-tier or peer-reviewed paper) sources OR ≥50 mixed (including OCW lecture notes)." Justify against the Kaggle ROGII modeling need.
