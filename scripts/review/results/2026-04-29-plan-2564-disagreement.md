### gemini

- Plan §Deliverable / YAML location states: "Create `digitalmodel/config/naval_architecture/yaw_moment_typical_ship.yml`" and later instructs to "add a test that `importlib.resources` or the selected loader can find the sample YAML". The proposed directory `digitalmodel/config/` is located at the repository root, outside the Python package tree (`digitalmodel/src/digitalmodel/`). `importlib.resources` can only resolve files from inside a valid Python module/package (e.g., `importlib.resources.files('digitalmodel.something')`). Placing the YAML outside the `src/` tree means it cannot be resolved by `importlib.resources`, leading to a guaranteed runtime failure and test failure.
- Plan §Citation Compliance claims: "`yaw_moment.py` emits/propagates a citation sidecar or metadata object matching `docs/standards/calc-output-citation.md`" for the "basic mechanics relation" and the "Whicker & Fehlner rudder lift model." The contract at `docs/standards/calc-output-citation.md` is strictly defined for "standards-derived numeric constants" and mandates 5 required fields (`code_id`, `publisher`, `revision`, `section`, `wiki_path`). Whicker & Fehlner is research literature, not an engineering standard. The plan attempts to force non-standard literature into a strict standard-compliance schema, which will either trigger a `ValidationError` at schema construction or require mocking fake publishers/code_ids, violating the integrity of the audit trail.
