- The review requested new automated tests for a prose-only proposal. Disposition: no executable implementation is introduced. Existing checks and source inspection verify the proposal; any new executable mechanism will require separately scoped TDD. Runtime installation remains gated on the stated existing-generator preservation checks.
- Print styling was incomplete. Disposition: page margins, repeated table headers, heading keep rules, widow/orphan controls and printed external URLs are added. - Fixed decimal places could imply physical precision. Disposition: the owner's three-decimal house convention is retained as display formatting only, with declared units and explicit separation from measurement/storage/solver accuracy. The review's proposed replacement metric tolerance is unsupported and is not adopted.
- Redaction could leave dangling internal citations. Disposition: each edition must reconcile citations to audience-permitted, revisioned document/model references and accessible evidence routes. Client-owned internal engineering references are not automatically forbidden.
- Positional finding numbers could break review persistence. Disposition: immutable identifiers stay in JSON metadata with report digest, quoted text and revision-specific section mapping; visible F-labels remain excluded under the latest owner instruction.
- The review requested a third heading level and claimed no compliance slot exists. Disposition: not adopted; two levels are explicit owner direction. Design Data contains acceptance criteria, Methodology contains governing methods and Results contains criterion-bound assessment. Detailed matrices may go in appendices without inventing a new top-level structure.
- The review requested a mandatory math encoding. Disposition: portable, legible offline equations and renderer verification are required; selection of a single new encoding would exceed this prose-consolidation scope and the unresolved renderer decision.
- The review alleged loss of valid baseline comparisons. Disposition: the existing text explicitly preserves valid comparators and distinguishes them from superseded results; archived evidence remains retained.
