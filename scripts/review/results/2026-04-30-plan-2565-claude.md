# Plan review #2565 — engineering/calculation correctness

Verdict: MAJOR on initial draft; remediated by plan revision.

Findings addressed:
1. Torque sign convention was underdefined; plan now emits hydrodynamic torque and equal/opposite steering gear holding torque.
2. Lever arm was underdefined; plan now requires perpendicular distance from stock axis to normal-force line of action and excludes center-of-pressure migration/stall/hull/propeller effects.
3. Tests were not non-tautological; plan now requires base-case absolute sign, negative-angle reversal, direct identity, and provenance tests.
4. Provenance now states the arm is user-supplied and no standards-derived torque coefficient is introduced.

Residual condition: implementation must follow the revised field names exactly.
