1. **The notification-purge fingerprint removal rests on an unverified byte-exact-match premise, and the plan presents no live-crontab evidence to close it.** Files to Change row for `config/workstations/harness-state-classes.yaml` plus Acceptance Criterion "exactly one managed `notification-purge` line appears in `plan['new_text']`" require *removing* the `preserved_local` fingerprint so the out-of-block line classifies `cataloged` instead of `preserved_external` (necessary because `classify_line` checks `preserved_external` before `cataloged` at `cron_transaction.py:193-198` — keeping the fingerprint yields a preserved copy **plus** a managed copy = two lines). The plan's reproduction probe (lines 135-142) greps for `WORKSPACE_HUB|LOG|REPO_ECOSYSTEM_HYGIENE_|repo-ecosystem-hygiene|cron-health` — it **never shows the live notification-purge line(s)**.
