The plan contains critical contradictions regarding frontmatter schema, arithmetic for ledger row updates, and false assertions about attested file existence.

- Missing `ledger_id` in pseudocode: The YAML frontmatter template omits the `ledger_id` key entirely, yet the Acceptance Criteria and Risks sections explicitly mandate its presence for `test_ledger_alignment` to bridge the gap between wiki-form and ledger-form IDs.
