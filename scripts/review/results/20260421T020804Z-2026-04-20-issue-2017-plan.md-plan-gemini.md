### Verdict: APPROVE

### Summary
The plan is comprehensive, well-structured, and clearly delineates responsibilities across different issues. The use of a local JSONL append-only log combined with an atomic snapshot is a highly pragmatic and robust approach to bypass current Gmail API scope limitations.

### Issues Found
- [P3] Minor: The 7-day grace period for transitioning threads from 'completed' to 'purged' appears to be hardcoded. It may be beneficial to expose this as a configurable parameter.
- [P3] Minor: While `fcntl.LOCK_EX` is robust for local filesystems, it may exhibit unexpected behavior if the repository is ever hosted on a network-attached filesystem (NFS/SMB).

### Suggestions
- Consider defining a log rotation or compaction strategy for `queue-state.jsonl` proactively, even if implementation is deferred, to ensure the unbounded growth risk is fully managed.
- Add explicit alerting or fallback mechanisms for the daily sweep cron job in case it repeatedly fails to acquire the lock.

### Questions for Author
- What is the expected behavior if the daily sweep script fails to acquire the lockfile within the 5-second timeout? Does it retry, or wait for the next day's cron execution?
- For the unknown-sender fallback, how will the pipeline handle potential spam that bypasses the Letstok rules but also doesn't match a known schema?
