Acceptance §295 will be unverifiable until this is pinned.

2. **`_write_markdown_artifact()` "policy-driven" rendering is decorative.** Plan §Pseudocode line 199 declares `_write_markdown_artifact()` "an explicit canonical renderer for this issue rather than a general section-order refactor" and inserts `filesystem_only_inventory` at a fixed position. Live `weekly_skills_audit.py:809-881` already calls each section by literal name, and the plan does not require the renderer to *read* `weekly_summary_sections.filesystem_only_inventory` to compute order. An implementer can satisfy every test with a single hard-coded if-branch that ignores the policy key entirely. The "policy-driven" framing in Acceptance §283 is unverifiable.

3. **`legacy_diverged_compatibility_total` ships dead weight into a stable recurring schema.** Plan §Pseudocode lines 173-175 and Acceptance §282 add this field to `inventory_summary.counts`. The snapshot at `docs/reports/issue-2488-planning-inventory-snapshot.json:12` records `"legacy_diverged_compatibility_total": 0` and `paths.legacy_diverged_compatibility: []`.
