## Findings

1. **Plan contract for YAML-driven section ordering is unenforceable against current code.** Plan §Files to Change row 1, §Pseudocode `run_weekly_audit_extension`, §Acceptance criterion #3, and §Risks bullet 4 assert that adding `filesystem_only_inventory` to `weekly_summary_sections` and `ranking_policy.section_order` will route the new section through "the existing policy loader and active-finding/Markdown rendering path so severity/reporting are policy-driven rather than bespoke constants." Verified: `_write_markdown_artifact` at `scripts/skills/weekly_skills_audit.py:809-879` hand-codes the section sequence with literal calls to `_append_finding_section(lines, "New Findings", ...)`, `..."Changed Findings"...`, etc., and never references `weekly_summary_sections` or `ranking_policy.section_order`. `_finding_defaults` *does* drive severity (line 343), but section *placement* does not. Implementation will silently fall back to hand-coding the new section regardless of YAML, and acceptance criterion #3 cannot be verified by inspecting YAML output alone. Pin one of: (a) implementation must refactor `_write_markdown_artifact` to read `ranking_policy.section_order`, OR (b) drop the YAML-drives-rendering claim and document hand-coded placement.

2. **"Sixth section" framing is off by four against actual rendered output.** Plan §Acceptance criterion #4 line 292 ("policy-ordered sixth `filesystem_only_inventory`") and §Pseudocode line 194 (same) count only the abstract `weekly_summary_sections` entries (5 → 6). Verified: `_write_markdown_artifact` at lines 829-879 produces ten visible H2 sections in the rendered Markdown (New Findings, Changed Findings, Content Quality, Grouping/Taxonomy Drift, Oversized/Maintainability, Usage/Staleness, Follow-up Candidates, Unresolved High-Confidence, Suppressed/Carry-Forward, Operational Errors). A reviewer checking "the sixth section is filesystem-only inventory" against actual output will not find what the plan describes. Either restate as "the sixth `weekly_summary_sections` policy entry" or count actual sections.

3. **Section placement instruction is ambiguous between two valid indices.** Plan §Pseudocode line 194 and §Files to Change row 6 (line 239) say "after `changed_findings` and before carry-forward sections". `config/skills/weekly-audit-policy.yaml:172-178` `ranking_policy.section_order` is `[new_findings, changed_findings, unresolved_high_confidence_findings, suppressed_carry_forward_findings, operational_errors_or_skipped_inputs]`. There are TWO entries between `changed_findings` and `suppressed_carry_forward_findings`. Implementation has at least three valid placements. Pin a single explicit predecessor (e.g., "immediately after `changed_findings`, immediately before `unresolved_high_confidence_findings`").

4. **New section has hybrid inventory + findings semantics with no rendering precedent.** Plan §Pseudocode line 194 says the section "list[s] every filesystem_only_active path regardless of ledger state" AND renders "high-signal unresolved findings ... through the same active-finding shape used by existing families" (acceptance #3). Existing helpers (`_append_finding_section` line 792, `_markdown_actionable_findings` line 779) take a list of finding dicts with `classification`, `finding_key`, `severity`, `confidence` keys (lines 797-802). Inventory paths have no such fields. The plan does not describe the row schema for inventory entries (do they synthesize a fake finding_key? do they live in a separate sub-list?). Specify the row shape or commit to a separate helper.

5. **Plan body cannot verify Codex/Claude/Gemini review summaries; the cited review-result files have `.err` siblings.** Plan §Adversarial Review Summary lines 313-315 paraphrase a Codex MAJOR verdict and Claude MINOR verdict whose details determine which revisions were applied.
