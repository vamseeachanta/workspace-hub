1. **MAJOR — `see_also` frontmatter field is fabricated; no schema or precedent.** Plan §Pseudocode line 192 emits `see_also: [≥2 sibling-page paths]`; Acceptance Criteria line 256 makes "≥2 `see_also` cross-links" a gate; TDD test `test_frontmatter_see_also_min_two` (line 241) hard-fails on its absence. 2. **MAJOR — Index-source-of-truth is unresolved at draft, with a known silent-revert hazard.** Plan §Risks line 293–294 explicitly says "`index.md` is regenerated periodically by `llm-wiki` tooling; manual edits may be overwritten ... write to `index.md` AND ... `knowledge/seeds/naval-architecture-resources.yaml` if that is the source of truth — **to be confirmed during plan review**." Resolve before implementation: either confirm `index.md` is hand-maintained for concept rows (cite the regenerator's contract), or move the canonical edit to the seeds yaml + add seeds-yaml to Files-to-Change.

3. **MAJOR — TDD `test_no_reservation_overlap` does not match the AC it tests, and the regex literal is broken.** Acceptance Criterion line 257 lists **five** reserved phrases: "turning circle, tactical diameter, advance, transfer, Nomoto". The TDD regex (line 243) is `r'\b(turning circle\|tactical diameter\|Nomoto)\b'` — three phrases, and the alternation operators are escaped pipes (`\|`) which Python `re` treats as literal `|` characters, not as alternation. Result: the test would only match the literal substring `turning circle|tactical diameter|Nomoto` — i.e., never trigger on real prose. This is a green-test fixture passing every page, regardless of content. Acceptance Criterion line 258 (no overlap with #2564/#2567 page scopes) has no test at all. The reservation-hygiene gate is the plan's load-bearing safety claim; both the AC↔test gap and the regex-syntax bug make it non-functional.

4. **MAJOR — Banning the substrings "advance" and "transfer" is over-broad and will collide with required content.** AC line 257 forbids any reference to "advance" and "transfer" in new pages. These are routine naval-architecture tokens: `concepts/ship-resistance-components.md` will discuss Froude advance coefficient; `concepts/propeller-theory.md` will discuss `J = Va/(nD)` (advance ratio) — a defining open-water quantity; `concepts/ship-weights-and-loading.md` will discuss longitudinal weight/buoyancy transfer. Substring-ban as currently specified would either force avoidance of canonical terminology (degrading the page) or the test would block merge on legitimate use. The #2568 reservation is the *workflow* (turning-circle / tactical-diameter / Nomoto-style estimator) — it does not lay claim to the bare nouns "advance" and "transfer" in propulsion/weights contexts. Either narrow the regex to multi-word phrases only ("advance and transfer" or "advance ratio of [a turning ship]") or drop "advance"/"transfer" from the ban-list and rely on the workflow-noun bans.
