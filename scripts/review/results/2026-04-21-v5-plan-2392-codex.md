### Verdict: MAJOR

### Summary
The plan still has correctness-critical gaps in the identity contract, source normalization contract, and approval evidence. It cannot be approved on the current draft because at least one stated no-false-gap property is contradicted by the matching rules, and multiple gap-count inputs remain underspecified or unverified.

### Issues Found
- `Identity Join Contract` is internally inconsistent and will create false gaps. The plan says source-side bare 64-hex values are normalized to `sha256:<hex>` (`Source-side compatibility reads`, step 2), but `Wiki-side coverage rule` says bare-hex wiki `doc_key` values are `diagnostic-only` and `never count as coverage` (steps 3-4). A source record and wiki page carrying the same legacy bare digest will therefore be reported as a `gap`, contradicting the stated goal to define the join contract `without false-gap behavior` and the claim `This avoids false positives during migration while still surfacing actionable backlog.`
- The optional join corpus is underspecified. `Authoritative source inputs` and `L3 Coverage Boundary` include `data/document-index/mounted-source-registry.yaml` and `data/document-index/online-resource-registry.yaml` as `supplemental join corpus`, but `Candidate Record Shape` and `Mapping expectations` define normalization only for `index-record`, `design-code`, `ledger`, and `promotion-manifest`. There is no record-shape or field-mapping contract for these two registries, yet the pseudocode feeds all supplemental matches into `normalize_source_records(...)`. That leaves gap counts and degraded behavior dependent on unspecified parser decisions.
- The approval gate depends on unverified live-state claims. `Authoritative source inputs` and `Manual verification before approval` require `data/document-index/index.jsonl`, and `Acceptance Criteria` requires the dry-run smoke check to `exit 0 and report run_status: clean on the approval target corpus`. The attested evidence does not verify `data/document-index/index.jsonl` exists and does not attest any clean run result. Because those claims are correctness-critical to approval, the plan has not affirmatively established that its required corpus or approval target is real.
- `domain-unresolved` is not implementable deterministically because the plan never defines what counts as a `valid output domain` or how domains are canonicalized into filenames. `Source record statuses` uses `domain-unresolved` when a record cannot be assigned to a `valid output domain`, `Deliverable` emits per-domain YAML, and `Files to Change` names outputs as `docs/reports/wiki-coverage-gaps/<domain>.yaml`. Without a normalization/slugging contract, the same logical domain can produce distinct files or invalid paths, and stale-file reconciliation cannot be verified.

### Suggestions
- Change the identity contract so legacy bare-hex source keys cannot become true gaps unless the matching wiki side has been evaluated under a symmetric migration rule. Either treat matching bare-hex wiki keys as `identity-migratable`/degraded coverage for this detector, or classify bare-hex source records as non-gap until both sides are canonicalized.
- Add explicit normalization specs for every supplemental source named in `supplemental join corpus`, including required fields, record type labels, join key extraction, domain derivation, and what happens when those files contain aggregate-only rows.
- Replace the current approval claim with verifiable gates: first attest `data/document-index/index.jsonl` existence, then require a recorded smoke-run artifact for the exact target checkout. Do not require `run_status: clean` unless the plan also defines how pre-existing wiki schema warnings/duplicates outside this issue are handled.
- Define a canonical domain identifier contract separate from display labels. Specify allowed characters, slugging rules, collision handling, and how `domain-unresolved` is determined before any file path is derived.

### Questions for Author
- Are bare-hex wiki `doc_key` pages supposed to suppress gaps during migration, or are you intentionally treating them as uncovered backlog? The current text says both.
- What exact schemas are expected for `mounted-source-registry.yaml` and `online-resource-registry.yaml`, and are they truly join-bearing inputs or only reporting aids?
