A manifest with full gates present will pass schema validation, yet that is explicitly NOT publication authorization. A naive consumer could read schema-valid as authorized. The fail-closed posture should be machine-enforced, not documented.

The plan hedges with 'if any structural path differs, implementation stops and the plan is revised' — defensive and good, but it means the plan may not be executable as written and could bounce back to planning mid-implementation.

This is a precondition/guard assertion against existing state, not a red-first behavior-driving test.
