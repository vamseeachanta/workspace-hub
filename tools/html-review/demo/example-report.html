Example Vessel Resistance Study

Engineering report · sample document for the review tool

1Executive Summary

A 20k DWT example tanker hull is analysed for calm-water resistance. The results are summarized in this section.

  • Scope. Eight loading conditions at three speeds.
  • Result. Resistance rises with draft and speed as expected for this hull form.

2Introduction

The purpose, scope and status of the study are presented in this section.

2.1Purpose

The study estimates bare-hull calm-water resistance over the operating drafts and trims of the example vessel.

3Design Data

The hull geometry, loading conditions and speeds used in the analysis are presented in this section.

3.1Hull and geometry

The hull is taken from the example CAD model. The principal particulars are an overall length of 150.0 m and a beam of 25.0 m.

3.3Speeds

The three analysis speeds are 11, 12 and 13 kn.

4Results

The resistance results are presented in this section.

Resistance increases by about 30 % from ballast to the loaded draft at 12 kn in this example.

5Conclusions

The conclusions of the example study are presented in this section.

The example results follow the expected trends with draft and speed. The values are illustrative only.
