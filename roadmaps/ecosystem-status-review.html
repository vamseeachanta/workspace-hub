Incident, 23 Nov 2026, about 11:38Z

WS014's desktop app showed "Couldn't connect to Claude, ERR_TIMED_OUT" and kept retrying. The WS014 intake desk stalled for as long as the app could not connect. Headless sessions on other machines kept working through the same period.

Decision rule

Intake and long-running desks run headless, in a terminal on a Linux machine. RDS02 is driven from ace-linux-2 one job at a time over SSH (claude -p or scripts, never --continue or --resume), and results are read back and logged on fleet-ops #577. The RDS02 intake desk is retired (owner, 20 Nov); ~/bin/rds02-intake.sh is retired with it.

Desktop machines are for review tabs and anything that needs a screen, not for critical desks.

Known failure points

Failure pointEffectWhat to do
Desktop app loses its connectionThe desk on that machine stalls until the app reconnects (23 Nov incident)Run critical desks headless; use the desktop app only for review tabs
Cross-session messages without delivery confirmationA message can be accepted and never readPost decisions and results on the fleet-ops issue; the issue is the record
A network mount that looks like a local folderDeleting through the mount deletes the files on the machine that owns the disk and frees nothing locallyCheck which machine owns a path (findmnt -T, readlink -f) before removing anything
A retired desk left in a schedulerThe scheduler keeps starting the desk on another account's allowance; a stale pass file can bring the old role backRetire a desk in its schedule as well as in its pass file
A session's permission check refuses a crontab or scheduler editA writer keeps running until someone with the right access pauses itPost the exact command as needs-owner; the owner runs pauses
