Incident, 23 Nov 2026, about 11:38Z

WS014's desktop app showed "Couldn't connect to Claude, ERR_TIMED_OUT" and kept retrying. The WS014 intake desk stalled for as long as the app could not connect. Headless sessions on other machines kept working through the same period.

Decision rule

Intake and long-running desks run headless, in tmux over SSH: on a Linux machine, or on RDS02 (Windows, Git Bash). RDS02 takes intake through ~/bin/rds02-intake.sh on ace-linux-2.

Desktop machines are for review tabs and anything that needs a screen, not for critical desks.
