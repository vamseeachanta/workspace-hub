`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`

**The industry's cloud+subscription consolidation is reshaping the competitive moat.** Sesam, SACS, and Flexcom all now offer cloud-gated analysis (overnight FEA runs on enterprise hardware). This makes expensive analysis cheaper for clients with subscriptions — a direct threat to local-desktop tool licensing. **Your response: lean into design-phase positioning.** aceengineer.com's lightweight calculators become MORE valuable as pre-cloud screening gates. "Run this design through our calc first (2 minutes, $0), then decide if it warrants $500+ cloud solver hours." This is the opposite of being disrupted by cloud — you become the funnel into it.

**Floating wind is now the industry's growth focus (50%+ of new capacity).** All three vendors (Bentley, DNV, Wood Group) are coupling riser/cable analysis to aerodynamic and platform dynamics. Your current v1.1 roadmap (OrcaWave on fixed platforms) is correct but outdated. Phase 999.2 should frontload floating-platform hydrostatic/stability modules — that's where the client demand will be in 2026–2027.

**OpenFAST maturation opens a research lane.** The shift to open-source wind energy simulation (especially floating offshore) means academic institutions now have a free, credible foundation. You can build partnership positioning: "digitalmodel's Python calc suite pairs with OpenFAST for open-source wind energy workflows." This differentiates from proprietary-tool-only vendors and wins research/early-stage project relationships.

**OrcaFlex remains the dynamic-analysis standard; no disruption there.** The modeless UI and pre-bend features are quality-of-life improvements, not architectural shifts. Your OrcaWave integration (v1.1) stays on the critical path. The competitive edge moves to **up-market service offerings** (consulting, design reviews, full FEA setups) bundled with your calc suite — not to outbuilding OrcaFlex.
