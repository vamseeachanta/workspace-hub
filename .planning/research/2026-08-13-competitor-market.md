The independent technical advisory group (established 2016) validates the integration methodology. This confirms the competitive convergence signal from August 6 research: all three major vendors (Bentley, DNV, Wood) now couple aerodynamics to structural systems for floating wind. → [Flexcom at Wood Group](https://www.woodgroup.com/solutions/expertise/flexcom)

2. **OpenFAST HydroDyn restructuring + SeaState wave module (2025–2026 development) improves hydrodynamic fidelity for floating platforms.** Wave-field generation separated into dedicated SeaState module; wave grid vertices now compute complete time-history incident waves + currents. New Keulegan-Carpenter (KC) dependent platform drag forces added. Development roadmap includes rotor flow confinement, new dynamic-stall models, and lifting loads on faired towers. Active ongoing work (September 2025 and beyond). → [Recent Hydrodynamic Modeling Enhancements in OpenFAST](https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1960423) | [OpenFAST GitHub Repository](https://github.com/OpenFAST/openfast)

3. **SACS 2026 R1 (March 2026): Direct Morph Workflow + GPU acceleration + Sherlock Electronics reliability integration.** Bentley's latest SACS release ships full Direct Morph Workflow (meshed geometry morphing without CAD round-trip), GPU-aware resource prediction for MAPDL, and integration with Sherlock electronics reliability analysis. Combined results database consolidates multi-phase design outputs into unified visualization. Cloud Services (Azure parallel compute) confirmed available for 10x+ load-case acceleration (per August 6 research). → [Ansys 2026 R1: Structural Mechanics](https://www.cadfem.net/en/cadfem-informs/newsroom/ansys-release/ansys-release-2026-structures.html) | [Bentley SACS Offshore Structure](https://www.bentley.com/software/sacs-offshore-structure/)

4. **Sesam via Veracity marketplace: Cloud compute services active, floating wind design focus, no public pricing changes announced (2026).** Sesam Wind Manager time-domain fatigue + ultimate strength analysis; Sesam Insight visualization; cloud FEA enabling parallel load-case runs in hours vs. days. COWI case study shows cloud pipeline reducing design timelines by up to 20x. Desktop Sesam licensing model appears unchanged; cloud gating via Veracity subscription. → [Sesam Cloud Compute — Veracity Marketplace](https://store.veracity.com/sesam-cloud-compute) | [COWI improves efficiency with Sesam cloud solutions](https://www.dnv.com/cases/cowi-156600/)

5. **Helica (DNV flexible pipe/umbilical module in Sesam): Cross-section fatigue + helix-wire analysis validated via JIP, performance benchmarked against full-scale umbilical tests (2016 validation).** Enables thousands of simulations in minutes vs. FEA hours per load case. No new 2026 updates announced; appears mature and stable. Integrated into Sesam ecosystem under floating wind + subsea expansion. → [Helica — Umbilical analysis](https://www.dnv.com/services/umbilical-analysis-and-flexible-pipe-analysis-helica-69553/)

`★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────`

**This week's search confirms the August 6 findings: competitive convergence around floating wind is real and accelerating.** Flexcom, SACS, Sesam, OpenFAST — all shipping production-grade aero-hydro-servo-elastic coupling in 2026. No single "new entrant" is disrupting the market; instead, the incumbents are converging on the same multi-physics stack.

**The strategic implication hasn't changed, but it's now validated:** Your lightweight design-phase calculators (OBS, wall thickness, fatigue, spectral response) become MORE valuable in this landscape, not less. Clients with $1–5k/month subscriptions to Flexcom/Sesam/SACS run detailed analyses, but they still need to screen designs first — and that's where digitalmodel's Phase 999.2 wind energy modules fit. The sales narrative is: "Run our hydrostatic + stability checks in 2 minutes, then decide if this design warrants the cloud-solver hours."

**Pricing remains stable (no disruption).** SACS Cloud (~$13.3k/yr), Sesam Cloud via Veracity (no public pricing, but similar SaaS model), Flexcom (traditional license, less common, bundled with OpenFAST research). This validates your decision to stay consultation-based (per PROJECT.md) — clients still prefer engineering judgment bundled with tools over raw software licensing. Validate this assumption with your next client.

**OpenFAST's continuing development (SeaState, KC-drag, rotor confinement) is the open-source signal.** This isn't a threat to commercial tools; it's the R&D foundation they build on. Academic institutions and early-stage projects use OpenFAST for proof-of-concept before buying Flexcom/Sesam. This opens a **research-partnership lane** (Phase 999.4 forward): digitalmodel + OpenFAST as the open-source design-phase stack, Flexcom/Sesam/SACS as the commercial refinement layer.
