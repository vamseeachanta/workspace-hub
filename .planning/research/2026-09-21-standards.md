# Research: standards — 2026-09-21

## Key Findings

1. **DNV Class Rules and Standards July 2026 Edition published — updated structural arrangement requirements with reduced corrosion margins for cargo tanks and simplified cut-out requirements in stiffeners; modernized direct analysis methodology for ship structure design.** Represents current baseline for DNV-classed vessel analysis. → [DNV July 2026 Standards Release](https://www.dnv.com/news/2026/standards-now-available-the-july-2026-edition-of-the-dnv-class-rules-and-standards-for-ship-and-offshore/)

2. **ISO 19901-1:2026 Metocean Design and Operating Considerations — confirmed as authoritative baseline for identification and characterization of meteorological, oceanographic, and environmental conditions (extreme, abnormal, long-term statistical distributions) applicable to all offshore structure types (fixed, floating, mobile, temporary).** Directly applicable to v1.1 OrcaWave hydrodynamic design-basis section. → [ISO 19901-1:2026 Standard](https://www.iso.org/standard/19901-1)

3. **ISO 25249 Corrosion Protection of Offshore Wind Structures — multi-part standard (5 parts: design, thermal spraying/painting, foundations, towers, nacelles) remains in Committee Draft stage, expected completion late 2026/early 2027.** Forward-looking framework for lifecycle-aware corrosion strategies; currently not normative but signals industry direction for subsea cable and foundation protection methodologies. → [ISO 25249 Development Status](https://www.iso.org/standard/89568.html) | [Institute of Corrosion: ISO Offshore Wind Corrosion Standards](https://www.icorr.org/new-iso-standards-on-corrosion-protection-of-offshore-wind-to-be-developed/)

4. **DNV subsea facilities standards remain authoritative framework — DNV-OS-E301 (position mooring), DNV-RP-C205 (environmental conditions/loads), DNV-OS-F201 (dynamic risers) cited as baseline for subsea mooring and pipeline design methodologies in recent OrcaFlex validation literature.** No 2026 updates detected; October 2010 editions remain current. → [DNV Subsea Standards](https://www.dnv.com/energy/services/subsea-facilities/world-class-codes/)

5. **API 579-1/ASME FFS-1 Fitness-For-Service — Parts 1–15 (metallic structures, Dec 2021 Edition) confirmed as normative baseline for pressure vessel, pipeline, and subsea equipment remaining-life assessment; Part 16 (FRP composites) expected Q4 2026 per prior research (no change this week).** Parts 5 (local metal loss) and 12 (dents/gouges) particularly relevant for subsea pipeline damage assessment. → [API 579 Fitness-for-Service Procedures](https://www.corrosionpedia.com/performing-a-fitness-for-service-assessment-of-pressure-vessels/2/7279) | [API 579 Inspectioneering Resource](https://inspectioneering.com/tag/api+579)
