# Research: standards — 2026-08-24

## Key Findings

1. **DNV-ST-N001 (Marine Operations) revised with 400+ industry comments incorporated — first major update in 5 years.** Enhanced guidance for design and planning of transport, installation, and removal of offshore wind farms, subsea cables, and oil & gas assets. The extended hearing window suggests significant industry pressure for clarity on installation procedures and risk mitigation in dynamic positioning, cable lay coordination, and vessel-interface management. → [DNV Updates Standard Used For Marine Operations For Offshore Wind, Subsea Cables And Oil & Gas Assets | Offshore Magazine](https://oceannews.com/news/energy/dnv-updates-standard-used-for-marine-operations-for-offshore-wind-subsea-cables-and-oil-gas-assets/) | [DNV-ST-N001 Marine Operations and Marine Warranty](https://www.dnv.com/energy/standards-guidelines/dnv-st-n001-marine-operations-and-marine-warranty/)

2. **DNV-ST-0359 (Subsea Power Cables) expanded to cover static AND dynamic cables with enhanced interface-management requirements (2026 edition).** Prior guidance focused on static shallow-water applications; expanded scope now addresses dynamic subsea cables (jumpers, dynamic export lines, umbilicals). Clearer design structure, refined load-envelope definitions, expanded cable-protection and installation-certification guidance. Directly applicable to Phase 999.2's floating wind mooring umbilical scope. → [DNV-ST-0359 Subsea Power Cables for Wind Power Plants](https://www.dnv.com/energy/standards-guidelines/dnv-st-0359-subsea-power-cables-for-wind-power-plants/) | [Windtech International — DNV Updates Subsea Cable Standard](https://www.windtech-international.com/product-news/dnv-updates-subsea-cable-standard-for-offshore-wind-applications)

3. **DNV July 2026 Edition introduces three new class notations: PE (power from external sources), CCW (carbon capture from well stream), and MIM (mooring integrity management).** The MIM notation specifically targets mooring-system compliance, audit trails, and predictive-maintenance documentation — directly relevant to Phase 999.2's floating wind mooring design and monitoring scope. → [Now available: The July 2026 edition of the DNV class rules and standards for ship and offshore](https://www.dnv.com/news/2026/standards-now-available-the-july-2026-edition-of-the-dnv-class-rules-and-standards-for-ship-and-offshore/) | [DNV Standards 2026: The New Benchmark for Offshore Wind and Green Energy Projects](https://www.inkl.com/news/dnv-standards-2026-the-new-benchmark-for-offshore-wind-and-green-energy-projects)

4. **ISO 19902 (Fixed Offshore Structures) increasingly paired with real-time digital SHM systems in 2026 practice — structural-integrity compliance now monitored continuously against ISO thresholds.** Updates emerging in practical integration: stress/deformation/corrosion indices captured from SHM feeds and cross-referenced against ISO 19902:2020 design limits. Implies v1.1 OrcaWave JSON outputs should emit SHM-native stress/reserve-factor fields for direct ingestion into monitoring systems. → [ISO 19902: Essential Guide for Fixed Offshore Structures](https://community.trustcloud.ai/article/iso-19902-everything-you-need-to-know/) | [Floating Offshore Wind and the Vessels That Support It — ABS](https://ww2.eagle.org/content/dam/eagle/publications/ABS_Floating_Offshore_Wind_and_the_Vessels_That_Support_It_2026.pdf)

5. **Lloyd's Register Recommended Practice for Floating Offshore Wind Turbine Support Structures (RP 2026) — emphasizes global standards convergence with DNV, ABS, and ISO for floating platforms.** LR's practice document cross-references ISO 19901 (metocean design) and ISO 19905 (pile/foundation design) alongside class rules. Signal: the industry is converging on a unified standards stack for floating wind, reducing site-specific rule interpretation. → [Recommended Practice for Floating Offshore Wind — Lloyd's Register](https://www.lr.org/en/knowledge/lloyds-register-rules/recommended-practice-for-floating-offshore-wind-turbine-support-structures/)
