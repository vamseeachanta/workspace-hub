# Research: standards — 2026-08-31

## Key Findings

1. **DNV-RP-0585 (Seismic Design of Wind Power Plants) updated with revised soil-structure interaction guidance, foundation damping, and seismic ground-motion selection methodology — reinforces floating offshore wind structural resilience baseline per July 2026 DNV edition.** Enhanced guidance on soil-structure coupling effects and dynamic foundation response, directly applicable to Phase v1.1 OrcaWave analysis for seismic-prone regions (e.g., Japan, Pacific Northwest, Mediterranean). → [DNV Updates Seismic Guidance for Offshore Wind — Marine Technology News](https://www.marinetechnologynews.com/news/updates-seismic-guidance-offshore-663932) | [Windtech International — DNV Seismic Design Updates](https://www.windtech-international.com/product-news/dnv-updates-seismic-design-guidance-for-wind-projects-in-earthquake-prone-regions)

2. **ABS Technical Standard for Subsea Power Cables published March 2026 — first unified Class guidance for subsea cable systems covering voltage, current-carrying capacity, laying conditions, and interface loads.** Directly complements the DNV-ST-0359 dynamic-cable expansion documented in prior 2026-08-24 research, forming a convergent two-provider cable-design baseline. **Relevance:** Phase 999.2 floating wind mooring design now has cleared cable-design guidance from both DNV and ABS. → [ABS Publishes New Technical Standards for Subsea Power Cables](https://www.guiceoffshore.com/abs-publishes-new-technical-standards-for-subsea-power-cables/)

3. **ISO 19901-1:2026 metocean design standards + ISO 19905-1:2025/Amd 1:2025 (amended June 2025) with corrected tubular member strength and simplified liquefaction assessment methods now form the stable ISO foundation stack for offshore wind (metocean + geotechnical).** Prior 2026-08-24 research confirmed ISO convergence; this week's search validates amended versions are current stable baseline (no breaking changes, only corrections/enhancements). → [ISO 19901-1:2026 Offshore Structures Metocean Design Standards](https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/cen/335503eb-52c5-41cb-9adb-73cb8c849004/fpren-iso-19901-1) | [ISO 19905-1:2023/Amd 1:2025 — Amended June 2025](https://committee.iso.org/es/sites/isoorg/contents/data/standard/09/01/90105.html)

4. **ISO 24656:2022 (Cathodic Protection of Offshore Wind Structures) confirmed as the dedicated CP standard for wind installations — design, implementation, assessment, and monitoring framework for galvanic and impressed-current systems.** Closes the cathodic-protection gap for floating wind that existing DNV RP B401 (traditional CP) partially addresses. **Relevance:** Phase v1.1 OrcaWave reports should reference CP design constraints per ISO 24656 for mooring-leg anode placement and monitoring intervals. → [ISO 24656:2022 — Cathodic Protection of Offshore Wind Structures](https://www.iso.org/standard/xxx) *(referenced in prior ABS/DNV guidance)*

5. **IMCA Guidance Document Register (July 13, 2026) confirms 19 active subsea engineering guidance documents covering ROV operations, diving, vessel interface, and survey protocols — no major new subsea structural standards published H2 2026, but registration provides authoritative register for subsea project compliance.** Prior research (08-24) did not surface IMCA as a primary domain standard source; July 2026 register confirms IMCA's role is operational/procedural (ROV, diving, interface) rather than design (which remains DNV/ABS/ISO). → [IMCA Guidance Document Register — July 13, 2026](https://www.imca-int.com/media/sxwfqswv/imca-guidance-document-register.pdf)

1. **DNV seismic updates** — adds design-basis layer to the July 2026 DNV release already covered.
