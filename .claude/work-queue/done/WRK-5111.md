## Mission

Transform the 20 bare stage-NN.md files into folder-skills (stage-NN-name/SKILL.md) with co-located contract.yaml, gotchas.md, and hooks.yaml. Distribute relevant gatepass sub-skill content into the appropriate stage folders.

## What / Why

The 20 stage micro-skills are bare .md files with no structure — contracts live separately in scripts/work-queue/stages/, operational lessons are scattered across gatepass sub-skills, and hooks are embedded in YAML. Anthropic's folder-skill convention (SKILL.md + scripts/ + references/) enables progressive disclosure and co-location. This conversion makes each stage self-contained and discoverable.
