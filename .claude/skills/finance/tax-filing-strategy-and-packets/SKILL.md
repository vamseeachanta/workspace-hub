# Tax Filing Strategy And Packets

## When to Use
Use when assembling tax filing packets, modeling C-Corp tax strategy, preparing TaxAct filing inputs, or reconciling personal/business tax documents.

## Class-Level Workflow
1. Separate strategic planning from packet assembly and software-entry navigation.
2. Preserve source-document provenance for each tax figure.
3. Create filing packets that are ready to enter/review rather than scattered notes.
4. Keep R&D and entity-structure strategy as advisory subcases under the same tax-planning umbrella.
