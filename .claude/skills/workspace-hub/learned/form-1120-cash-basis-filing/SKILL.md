# Form 1120 Cash-Basis Filing from Expense Sheet

When preparing Form 1120 for a cash-method C-Corp without prior-year returns, use the expense sheet (bank-activity view) as authoritative source of truth for revenue and expenses. Map each expense line to corresponding Form 1120 line items, then coordinate Schedule J (tax computation), Schedule L (balance sheet with reconstructed beginning balances), Schedule K (entity info), and Schedule M-1 (book-tax reconciliation). Identify critical gaps (bank balances, capital stock, retained earnings, estimated payments) before filing. Note: FreeTaxUSA does not support Form 1120; use IRS PDF, Otto, or PdfFiller instead.
