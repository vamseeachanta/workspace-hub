# Corporate Tax Filing Reconciliation

When reconciling a corporate tax filing packet with multiple authoritative sources (worksheets, 1099s, depreciation schedules, cost-seg studies) under time pressure, establish a phased verification workflow: (1) Confirm filing identity and EIN across all documents; (2) Cross-reference 1099 Box amounts, payer/recipient TINs, and account numbers against master worksheets; (3) Locate all referenced support documents (cost-seg studies, depreciation schedules) and verify they align with filing strategy; (4) Check for corrections/amendments noted on forms; (5) Make binary go/no-go filing decision based on reconciliation gaps. This prevents last-minute filing errors on multi-form returns (1120, 8825, 4562).
