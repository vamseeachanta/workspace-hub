## Operating principles (apply to every skill here)
1. **Outcomes over vanity** — followers/likes are leading indicators; the goal is
   conversations → registrations/RFQs → won work.
2. **Warm-first, never spray** — ICP-gate every list; cold mass-targeting is a background track.
3. **One ask per touch**, value before the ask, a logged next step for every contact.
4. **Honest posture** — never imply work, vendor status, or claims that don't exist.
5. **Public/PII hygiene** — keep client names, contacts, and figures out of public/shared
   surfaces; real data lives in the private engagement repo.
6. **Every target needs a tracking mechanism** — instrument every funnel hook.
