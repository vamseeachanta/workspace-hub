Companion to [[feedback_vamsee_email_style_skestates]] (that one = real-estate vendor threads;
this one = technical prospect/lead outreach, e.g. Reed Goodman [[project_reed_goodman_collide_lead]]).

**Learned 2026-07-05** by comparing my staged follow-up draft to Reed vs the version Vamsee
actually sent. The edits are the signal:

1. **Thread into the EXISTING conversation — don't invent a new subject.** Reed had a prior direct
   thread (`Low BHP | Well Analysis`, 2026-07-03). Vamsee sent the follow-up as a **reply** into it;
   my draft had a fresh subject ("Following up from Collide…"). **BEFORE drafting any follow-up,
   `search_threads` by the recipient's ADDRESS (`to:<email>` + in:sent) to find a live thread, and
   reply into it (`replyToMessageId`).** I missed Reed's thread by searching the topic ("Reed/Collide")
   instead of `to:Chief@warturkey.com`.
2. **Cut the claim/validation sentence; keep it humble + forward-looking.** He deleted "the onshore
   screen backs up exactly what you and Martin were seeing" — the one sentence asserting a conclusion.
   Same instinct as the skestates edits (drop assertions, keep constructive next steps).
3. **Every capability mentioned gets a LIVE indexable link.** He added the dynacard page URL
   (`…/digitalmodel/artificial-lift/dynacard-troubleshooting.html`) to the rod-pump P.S. — which is why
   he kept asking "where is the dynacard html." A text-only capability offer reads as unfinished to him;
   BUILD/publish the page and link it ([[feedback_unique_live_links_traffic_credibility]]).
4. **Expand the generosity — offer more help, warmly.** He added a whole paragraph offering near-real-time
   drilling/completion analytics ("overnight followed by real-time … help the crew with way forward, risk
   assessments … Just howler and I am here to help") and closed "help in any way possible." My draft was
   more clipped. His voice here is warmer/more colloquial than the formal vendor voice.
5. **Personalize the greeting** with the recipient's handle/nickname — "Chief Reed," (from Chief@warturkey.com),
   not just "Reed,".
6. **Frame assets as living** — "(live — I keep adding to it)" over "(browsable, all areas)".

**Signature (technical outreach):** Vamsee Achanta / AceEngineer / +1 713-306-9029.

**How to apply:** for a prospect follow-up, FIRST find & reply into the existing thread; open with a
personalized greeting; state what you did + give live links (one per capability, build the page if missing);
drop conclusion-claims; end with expansive, informal offers of further help + one concrete ask (well name /
location / API for analog assessment). Keep the honest-data discipline from the artifacts themselves.
