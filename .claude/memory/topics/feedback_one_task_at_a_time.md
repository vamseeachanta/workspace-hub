After completing work, end the turn with a concise **summary** and a **call to action**, then surface exactly **ONE next task** — not a menu of 3–4 options to choose from.

**Why:** the user steers a sequence one step at a time; multi-option closings ("your call on next steps: A / B / C / D") are too much. They'll redirect if the proposed next task isn't the one they want.

**How to apply:** pick the single highest-value, well-scoped next step and present it with a clear proceed/redirect ask. Hold the rest of the backlog in reserve (mention it exists in one line if useful), and reveal the next one only after the current task is done.
